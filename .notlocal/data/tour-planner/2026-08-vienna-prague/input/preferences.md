## Must-Visit (non-negotiable)

- St. Stephen's Cathedral (free interior; South Tower for better view, North Tower has elevator)
- Hofburg Palace / Sisi Museum (9 AM – 5:30 PM, last entry 4:30 PM)
- Belvedere Palace — Upper Belvedere for Klimt's Kiss (9 AM – 6 PM, last entry 5:30 PM)
- Schönbrunn Palace (timed entry ticket required)
- Prater / Vienna Giant Ferris Wheel
- Donaukanal Cruise (75 minutes; https://ddsg-blue-danube.at/cruises-vienna/city-cruise/?lang=en)
- Charles Bridge, Prague
- Prague Castle (Circuit B tickets)
- Astronomical Clock, Old Town Square, Prague

## Nice-to-Have (include if time allows)

- Walk along the Ringstrasse (circular road around Vienna's historic center)
- Vienna State Opera (Wiener Staatsoper) — exterior / quick look
- Day trip to Bratislava, Slovakia (only if an extra day opens up by skipping Hofburg)

## Day Assignments (honor if feasible)

- 2026-08-08: St. Stephen's Cathedral + Hofburg Palace + walk around historic center / Ringstrasse
- 2026-08-09: Schönbrunn Palace + Ferris Wheel (Prater)
- 2026-08-10: Belvedere Palace + Donaukanal river cruise

## Scheduling Hints

- Prater / Ferris Wheel: evening activity — it's 1 km from accommodation, perfect for low-energy end of day
- Palaces: morning slots to beat heat and crowds
- Donaukanal Cruise: pair with Belvedere day since both are on the same side of the city

## Booking Preferences

- Prefer official ticket websites over third-party resellers
- For Prague Castle: Circuit B tickets (cheaper, not timed, non-cancellable is acceptable)
- Schönbrunn: timed entry required — book early morning slot

Avoid thin-soled shoes or heels.Supportive sandals: Great for hot days, but choose pairs with thick, sturdy soles.Blister pads or moleskin: Pack these in your day pack just in case.

Daytime Clothing (Light & Breathable)5–7 lightweight tops: Bring breathable cotton, linen, or moisture-wicking t-shirts.2–3 pairs of shorts or skirts: Perfect for daytime sightseeing.2 pairs of lightweight trousers: Linen pants or light chinos are excellent for days when you want sun protection or coverage.1 dressy/semi-formal outfit: Vienna is known for classical concerts and historic venues (like the Opera). Bring a nice dress, collared shirt, or smart trousers if you plan to attend.1 light scarf or pashmina: Crucial for quickly covering shoulders or knees when entering religious sites like St. Stephen’s Cathedral.
