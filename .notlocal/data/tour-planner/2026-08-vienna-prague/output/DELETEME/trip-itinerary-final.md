### 🏙️ City Overviews

🇦🇹 **Vienna** — The "City of Music" is grand, imperial, and surprisingly green. Staying at Fugbachgasse 17 in Leopoldstadt gives you easy U1 access from Nestroyplatz (5-min walk), proximity to the Prater (1 km walk), and the excellent Karmelitermarkt for breakfasts. The Ringstrasse — Vienna's grand circular boulevard encircling the historic center — is approximately 5.3 km total. A partial walk along the most scenic stretch (Opera → Parliament → Rathaus → Burgtheater → back to Stephansplatz) is ~2.5 km / 35 minutes and is fully stroller-friendly with wide, flat sidewalks and tram tracks alongside.

🇨🇿 **Prague** — Known as the "City of a Hundred Spires." Your base at Plaská in Malá Strana is quiet, family-friendly, at the foot of Petřín Hill, steps from Kampa Island, and 1 minute from the Tram 12/22 stop at Újezd.

**Recommended transit passes:**
- Vienna: 72-hour Vienna City Card (€29/adult; under 15 free on Sundays/holidays/Vienna school holidays — includes August)
- Prague: 72-hour Lítačka pass (120 CZK/adult; under 6 free, 6–15 half price)

### Day 2: 🏛️ The Heart of Old Vienna (Saturday, August 8)

☀️ **31 °C, sunny.** Hydration is key. Indoor focus during midday heat. Outdoor walks early morning and evening.

📍 [Today's Route Map](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Fugbachgasse+17+Wien/Stephansdom+Wien/Hofburg+Palace+Wien/Cafe+Central+Wien/Naturhistorisches+Museum+Wien/Fugbachgasse+17+Wien)

🏛️ 9:00 AM: St. Stephen's Cathedral. Take U1 from Nestroyplatz to Stephansplatz (2 stops, 4 min). The gothic interior is cool and free to enter (6 AM–10 PM). The South Tower (136m) offers the best panoramic view of Vienna but requires climbing 343 steps — manageable for the 7-year-old. The North Tower has elevator access and houses the Pummerin bell — better choice with the toddler and stroller.
👶 Kid-friendly: 3/5 | 🚼 Stroller: Yes (ground floor, North Tower elevator)
💰 Free (cathedral); € (tower ~€7/adult, under 15 half price)

🚶 10:00 AM: Stroll along Graben & Kohlmarkt. Wide pedestrian boulevards, street performers. Grab gelato at Zanoni & Zanoni — Vienna's best, huge portions, open late.

🏛️ 10:45 AM: Hofburg Palace / Sisi Museum. Enter from Michaelerplatz (5-min walk from Graben). The Imperial Apartments and Sisi Museum trace Empress Elisabeth's fascinating life. Silver Collection on ground floor is quick and stroller-accessible. Allow 1.5 hours. Last entry 4:30 PM.
🎟️ BOOK AHEAD: Not strictly required but online tickets skip the queue. [Book tickets here](https://www.imperial-austria.at/tickets.html)
👶 Kid-friendly: 3/5 (better for 7-year-old; toddlers in stroller)
🚼 Stroller: Yes (elevator available) | 💰 Cost: €€ (under 6 free)

🍽️ 12:30 PM: Lunch at Café Central. Iconic Viennese coffeehouse, 5-min walk from Hofburg via Herrengasse. Arrive by 12:30 to avoid the longest queue.
🌟 Must-try: Apfelstrudel with vanilla sauce, Kaiserschmarrn
👶 Kid-friendly: 4/5 | 💰 Cost: €€
No reservations (first come).

🚶 1:45 PM: Ringstrasse Walk. Exit Café Central and head south to the Ring. Walk the scenic stretch: Parliament → Rathaus (City Hall) → Burgtheater → Volksgarten. This is ~1.5 km, about 25 minutes at family pace, entirely flat and stroller-friendly. Wide tree-lined sidewalks with benches. *Answer to your question: Yes, you can absolutely walk the Ringstrasse with a stroller — the sidewalks are wide, flat, and shaded by trees. The full ring is 5.3 km (~70 min) but the highlights are packed into the 1.5 km western stretch.*

🎨 2:30 PM: Naturhistorisches Museum. Enter from Maria-Theresien-Platz (you'll pass it on the Ring walk). Dinosaurs, meteorites, gems — perfect escape from midday heat. Under 19 is FREE. Air-conditioned.
👶 Kid-friendly: 5/5 | 🚼 Stroller: Yes
💰 €€ (adults only — under 19 free)

🏨 4:30 PM: Return to hotel for toddler rest. U2 from Museumsquartier → Praterstern, walk home (20 min total).

🍽️ 6:00 PM: Dinner at Gasthaus Wild. Traditional Viennese, 8-min walk from flat. Schnitzel, Tafelspitz, good children's portions.
👶 Kid-friendly: 4/5 | 💰 Cost: €€
Reservations recommended.

🚶 7:00 PM: Evening stroll along the Donaukanal. Street art, boats, kids run on the towpath. 8 min from hotel. Grab ice cream at the Eissalon am Schwedenplatz on the way back.

### Day 3: 🏰 Imperial Splendor & Evening Fun (Sunday, August 9)

☀️ **33 °C, sunny.** Hottest day — early start critical. Outdoor before 11 AM, indoor/shaded midday, Prater in the cooler evening.

📍 [Today's Route Map](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Fugbachgasse+17+Wien/Schönbrunn+Palace+Wien/Brandauers+Schlossbräu+Wien/Fugbachgasse+17+Wien/Wiener+Riesenrad)

🏰 9:00 AM: Schönbrunn Palace. Take U1 from Nestroyplatz → Karlsplatz, transfer U4 → Schönbrunn (20 min total). Book the 9:00 AM Grand Tour to beat crowds and heat.
🎟️ BOOK AHEAD: Schönbrunn uses timed entry and sells out in summer. Book 2–3 weeks ahead. [Book tickets here](https://www.schoenbrunn.at/en/tickets-and-prices/all-tickets-tours)
👶 Kid-friendly: 4/5 | 🚼 Stroller: Yes (gardens), partial (inside — elevator available)
💰 Cost: €€

🌳 10:15 AM: Schönbrunn Gardens & Maze. Let kids run in the Privy Garden. The maze and Labyrinth playground are a hit for the 7 and 4-year-olds. Crown Prince Garden is a quiet hidden gem nearby. Free entry.

🎨 11:30 AM: Children's Museum (inside palace). Air-conditioned. Kids dress up as tiny emperors. Interactive exhibits about daily life in the imperial court.

🍽️ 12:30 PM: Lunch at Brandauers Schlossbräu. Beer garden 3-min walk from Hietzinger Tor exit. Shaded, casual, kid-friendly.
🌟 Must-try: Schnitzel, Kaiserschmarrn
💰 Cost: €€. No reservations needed.

🏨 2:00 PM: Return to hotel for rest. U4 + U1 back (25 min). Toddler nap / quiet time. Hydrate, cool down.

🛍️ 4:00 PM: Karmelitermarkt. 4-min walk from flat. Sunday market — local food stalls, organic produce, Vietnamese, falafel, pastries. Snack and browse.

🎡 5:30 PM: Wiener Riesenrad (Giant Ferris Wheel). Walk to Prater (1 km from your flat, 12 min). Iconic Vienna panorama at golden hour — the "Third Man" wheel. Temperature drops to ~28 °C by now.
🎟️ BOOK AHEAD: Purchase flexible day tickets online to skip lines. [Book tickets here](https://wienerriesenrad.com/en/tickets/)
👶 Kid-friendly: 5/5 | 🚼 Stroller: Yes
💰 Cost: €€

🎡 6:15 PM: Wurstelprater rides. Age-appropriate carousel, bumper cars, miniature railway. The Liliputbahn is a hit. Open until 11 PM in summer.

🍽️ 7:00 PM: Dinner at Schweizerhaus. Classic Bohemian beer garden inside the Prater. Enormous Stelze (pork knuckle), Budvar on tap, Erdäpfelpuffer for kids. Huge outdoor seating — kids can roam safely.
🌟 Must-try: Stelze (pork knuckle) — enormous and crispy
👶 Kid-friendly: 5/5 (huge outdoor space) | 💰 Cost: €€
No reservations.

🚶 8:00 PM: Walk home through Prater Hauptallee (10-min walk back to Fugbachgasse via Ausstellungsstraße). The tree-lined avenue is beautiful at dusk.

### Day 4: 🎨 Art, Gardens & River Cruise (Monday, August 10)

⛅ **30 °C, hazy sun, possible afternoon thunderstorm.** Outdoor morning, cruise midday (shaded on water), flexible afternoon.

📍 [Today's Route Map](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Fugbachgasse+17+Wien/Belvedere+Palace+Wien/Schweizergarten+Wien/DDSG+Schiffstation+Wien/Fugbachgasse+17+Wien)

🏛️ 9:30 AM: Upper Belvedere. Tram 2 from Taborstraße to Quartier Belvedere (15 min). Klimt's "The Kiss," baroque gardens with fountains and city panorama. Under 19 free.
👶 Kid-friendly: 3/5 (paintings) / 5/5 (gardens)
💰 €€ (adults only — under 19 free)

🌳 11:00 AM: Walk through Belvedere Gardens to the lower palace. Fountains, sphinx statues, shaded paths. Photo at the reflecting pool with the palace behind you.

☕ 11:30 AM: Coffee & snack break at Café Oberlaa (inside Lower Belvedere orangery). Excellent pastries, shaded terrace.

🚶 12:00 PM: Walk to Schweizergarten park (5 min from Lower Belvedere). Playground for kids, shaded benches for adults. Let kids burn energy before the cruise.

🍽️ 12:45 PM: Lunch at Gasthaus Pöschl. Traditional Viennese tavern near Schwedenplatz (U4 from Südtiroler Platz → Schwedenplatz, 8 min). Goulash, Wiener Schnitzel. Quick and hearty.
👶 Kid-friendly: 4/5 | 💰 Cost: €€
No reservations needed.

🚢 2:00 PM: Donaukanal City Cruise (75 minutes). Board at Schwedenplatz DDSG station (2-min walk from lunch). The cruise goes along the Donaukanal past street art, the Urania observatory, and into the main Danube with views of the UNO City skyline. Covered upper deck with open-air option. *Answer to your question: Yes, the cruise is suitable for a 2-year-old — the boat is smooth and slow, there's covered seating, a small bar for drinks/snacks, and toddlers can stand at the railing safely (glass panels). No age restriction.*
👶 Kid-friendly: 5/5 | 🚼 Stroller: Yes (flat boarding ramp)
💰 Cost: €€ (~€27/adult, under 10 free)

🏨 3:30 PM: Return to hotel. Walk from Schwedenplatz along the canal back home (15 min) or U1 from Schwedenplatz → Nestroyplatz (2 min). Rest, nap time.

🍽️ 6:00 PM: Dinner at Tempel. Modern Austrian with Middle Eastern touches, 5-min walk from flat (Praterstraße). Shareable plates, excellent hummus, grilled lamb.
👶 Kid-friendly: 4/5 | 💰 Cost: €€
Reservations recommended.

🚶 7:00 PM: Final evening stroll to Donaukanal for sunset. Say goodbye to Vienna. Pack for tomorrow's train.

### Day 5: 🚇 Transit to Prague (Tuesday, August 11)

⛅ **Vienna 28 °C morning; Prague 26 °C afternoon, partly cloudy.**

📍 [Today's Route Map](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Fugbachgasse+17+Wien/Naschmarkt+Wien/Wien+Hauptbahnhof/Praha+hlavní+nádraží/Plaská+622+Praha)

🛍️ 8:30 AM: Quick visit to Naschmarkt. U1 from Nestroyplatz → Karlsplatz (6 min). Grab pastries, fresh juice, and fruit for the train ride. Browse a few stalls — the market is 1.5 km long but the best food stalls are in the first 300m from Karlsplatz end.

🚇 9:45 AM: Head to Wien Hauptbahnhof. U4 Kettenbrückengasse → U1 to Wien Hbf (10 min). Arrive by 10:15 AM. Buy snacks at Anker bakery on platform.

🚇 11:10 AM: ÖBB Railjet Train to Prague. 4-hour ride. Pack coloring books, tablet with downloads, snacks. Book "Family Zone" seats for the play area.
🎟️ BOOK AHEAD: ÖBB family seats sell out in summer. Book 1 month ahead. [Book tickets here](https://www.oebb.at/en/)

🏨 3:23 PM: Arrive Praha hlavní nádraží. Metro A (green) to Malostranská (4 stops, 8 min). Walk downhill to Plaská (8 min). Check in by 4:30 PM.

🌳 5:15 PM: Stroll through Kampa Island. 5-min walk from flat. Peaceful park along the Vltava river, the "Venice of Prague" canal, giant baby statues by David Černý. Great for kids to stretch legs after the train.

🍽️ 6:15 PM: Dinner at Café Savoy. Right around the corner from your flat (3-min walk). Czech-French brasserie under a neo-Renaissance ceiling. Own bakery — the pastries are legendary.
🌟 Must-try: Duck confit, Medovník (honey cake)
👶 Kid-friendly: 4/5 | 💰 Cost: €€
Reservations essential.

### Day 6: 🏰 Castles & Views (Wednesday, August 12)

🌧️ **25 °C, pleasant with 40% chance of afternoon showers.** Pack rain jackets. Indoor-heavy morning (castle), flexible afternoon.

📍 [Today's Route Map](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Plaská+622+Praha/Prague+Castle/Villa+Richter+Praha/Petřín+Lookout+Tower/Lokál+U+Bílé+kuželky+Praha/Plaská+622+Praha)

🏰 9:00 AM: Prague Castle. Take Tram 22 from Újezd directly to Pražský hrad (7 min, scenic ride). Enter from the west (Hradčanské náměstí) for the shortest security queue. St. Vitus Cathedral (breathtaking stained glass by Mucha), Old Royal Palace, Golden Lane with armor displays and Kafka's tiny house #22.
🎟️ BOOK AHEAD: Prague Castle is busiest 10 AM–2 PM in summer. Buy Circuit B tickets online 1 week ahead. Circuit B covers St. Vitus Cathedral, Old Royal Palace, St. George's Basilica, and Golden Lane — best value for families. [Book tickets here](https://www.hrad.cz/en/prague-castle-for-visitors/tickets)
👶 Kid-friendly: 4/5 | 🚼 Stroller: partial (cobblestones, stairs — bring carrier for toddler)
💰 Cost: €€ (under 6 free)

🌳 11:30 AM: Castle Gardens viewpoint. Panoramic photo over Prague's rooftops and the Vltava. Walk down through the South Gardens (free, beautiful terraced descent).

🍽️ 12:00 PM: Lunch at Villa Richter. 5-min walk from castle east gate. Terrace in a working vineyard with panoramic views over the city. Grilled meats, fresh salads.
👶 Kid-friendly: 4/5 | 💰 Cost: €€
Reservations recommended.

🎡 1:30 PM: Petřín Hill. Walk downhill from castle area through Strahov Gardens to Petřín (15 min). Or take the funicular from Újezd (near your flat). Mirror Maze is a highlight for the 7 and 4-year-olds. The lookout tower (mini Eiffel Tower, 63m) offers stunning city views.
👶 Kid-friendly: 5/5 | 🚼 Stroller: Yes (funicular, paved paths)
💰 € (funicular = transit pass; tower + maze ~250 CZK total)

🌳 3:00 PM: Petřín Gardens playground. Large playground on grassy slopes. Toddler can nap in stroller while older kids play. If rain starts, take the funicular down (2 min to your flat from Újezd stop).

🏨 4:00 PM: Return to hotel for rest. 2-min walk from Újezd funicular station.

🍽️ 6:00 PM: Dinner at Lokál U Bílé kuželky. 4-min walk from flat (Újezd 12). Authentic Czech pub, fresh tank Pilsner Urquell, comfort food kids love. Energetic but welcoming.
🌟 Must-try: Svíčková na smetaně (braised beef with cream sauce and dumplings — the Czech national dish)
👶 Kid-friendly: 5/5 | 💰 Cost: €
Reservations recommended.

### Day 7: 🏛️ Old Town & Vyšehrad (Thursday, August 13)

⛅ **27 °C, partly cloudy.** Comfortable outdoor day — maximize time outside.

📍 [Today's Route Map](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Plaská+622+Praha/Charles+Bridge+Praha/Old+Town+Square+Praha/Lokál+Dlouhááá+Praha/Vyšehrad+Praha/Plaská+622+Praha)

🚶 8:45 AM: Charles Bridge. 8-min walk from flat. Cross early to beat crowds heading east. 30 baroque statues line the bridge. Touch the brass dog on St. John of Nepomuk statue for luck (5th statue on the right). Views of the castle behind you.
👶 Kid-friendly: 4/5 | 🚼 Stroller: Yes (flat stone surface)
💰 Free

🏛️ 9:15 AM: Old Town Square & Astronomical Clock. The clock chimes every hour with its procession of apostles — try to catch the 10:00 AM show. Buy a warm Trdelník from a stand — kids love the cinnamon chimney cake. Explore Týn Church courtyard.
👶 Kid-friendly: 5/5 | 💰 Free

🏛️ 10:15 AM: Jewish Quarter (Josefov). 5-min walk north from Old Town Square. Old Jewish Cemetery (hauntingly beautiful, 12,000 tombstones layered), Old-New Synagogue (Europe's oldest active synagogue, 1270), Spanish Synagogue (stunning Moorish interior). Keep to 1.5 hrs max with young kids.
👶 Kid-friendly: 3/5 (historical; good for the 7-year-old)
💰 €€ (combined ticket ~500 CZK/adult; under 6 free)

🚶 11:45 AM: Walk along Pařížská street. Prague's most elegant boulevard — Art Nouveau buildings, window shopping.

🍽️ 12:15 PM: Lunch at Lokál Dlouhááá. 5-min walk from Josefov. Same excellent Czech pub group as yesterday. Fresh tank beer, classic comfort food.
🌟 Must-try: Vepřo-knedlo-zelo (roast pork, bread dumplings, sauerkraut)
👶 Kid-friendly: 5/5 | 💰 Cost: €
Reservations recommended.

🌳 2:00 PM: Vyšehrad Fortress. Metro B from Náměstí Republiky → Florenc → C to Vyšehrad (20 min total). Ancient hilltop fortress with stunning river views, far fewer tourists than the Castle. Cemetery with Dvořák's grave, Romanesque rotunda, casemates. The playground here is one of Prague's best — massive, modern, built into the hillside.
👶 Kid-friendly: 5/5 | 🚼 Stroller: Yes (main paths)
💰 Free

☕ 4:00 PM: Playground & ice cream. Let all three kids play freely at the Vyšehrad playground. Kiosk for treats and drinks.

🚇 5:00 PM: Return to hotel. Tram 3/17 from Výtoň → Újezd (10 min direct). 1-min walk home.

🍽️ 6:00 PM: Dinner at U Kroka. Modern Czech, outstanding roast duck. Small bistro (20 seats) in a quiet Malá Strana side street, 5-min walk from flat.
🌟 Must-try: Roast duck with red cabbage and potato dumplings
👶 Kid-friendly: 4/5 | 💰 Cost: €€
Reservations essential.

### Day 8: 🌳 Letná Park & Farewell Malá Strana (Friday, August 14)

🌤️ **25 °C, clear.** Pleasant outdoor day — last full day. Relaxed pace.

📍 [Today's Route Map](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Plaská+622+Praha/Letná+Park+Praha/Café+Letka+Praha/Malá+Strana+Praha/Augustine+Restaurant+Praha/Plaská+622+Praha)

🌳 9:30 AM: Letná Park. Tram 12/22 from Újezd → Letenské náměstí (10 min). Walk to the Metronome viewpoint — THE best panorama in Prague (all bridges visible below, castle to the left).
👶 Kid-friendly: 5/5 | 🚼 Stroller: Yes
💰 Free

🌳 10:00 AM: Letná Playground. Large, well-equipped. Good climbing structures and swings.

☕ 10:45 AM: Letná Beer Garden. Draft Pilsner for adults, juice for kids. Panoramic terrace with the iconic view.

🍽️ 11:30 AM: Lunch at Café Letka. In the Letná neighborhood. Excellent brunch-style menu, good coffee, kid-friendly with high chairs.
💰 Cost: €€. No reservations needed.

🚶 1:00 PM: Walk back to Malá Strana. Cross Čechův Bridge → Malostranská → through Malá Strana streets (20 min, scenic downhill walk past embassies and baroque palaces).

🛍️ 1:30 PM: Souvenir shopping on Nerudova. Leading up toward the castle — wooden toys, marionettes, Bohemian crystal. House signs (At the Two Suns, At the Red Eagle) are charming.

🌳 2:30 PM: Vrtba Garden. Hidden baroque garden with terraced city views (Karmelitská 25). Quiet and beautiful — one of Prague's UNESCO-listed gardens. ~100 CZK entry.

☕ 3:30 PM: Final Trdelník & coffee at a Malá Strana café. Walk back to hotel (5 min). Rest and pack.

🍽️ 5:30 PM: Farewell dinner at Augustine Restaurant. 6-min walk (Letenská 12). Modern Czech cuisine in a former Augustinian monastery. Brewery heritage with St. Thomas dark beer brewed on premises.
🌟 Must-try: Beef cheeks, St. Thomas monastery beer
👶 Kid-friendly: 4/5 | 💰 Cost: €€–€€€
Reservations recommended.

🚶 7:00 PM: Sunset walk across Charles Bridge. Far fewer people in the evening — magical golden light on the statues, castle illuminated behind you. Say goodbye to Prague. Return to flat and pack. Set alarms for early departure.

🌤️ **24 °C, pleasant.**

✈️ 8:15 AM: Arrive at Prague Airport (PRG). Check in and settle into family lanes.

### 🎒 Packing Tips

🚼 Lightweight stroller with large wheels. Cobblestones in both cities are relentless on small wheels.
🎒 Baby carrier/backpack. Essential for Prague Castle stairs and Vyšehrad hillside.
🌧️ Packable rain jackets. Summer showers hit fast in both cities.
☀️ Sun hats + SPF 50 sunscreen. Vienna reaches 33 °C in August.
💧 Refillable water bottles. Vienna has free public drinking fountains everywhere (look for green hydrants).
🍪 Small daypack with snacks. Toddler meltdown prevention.
🎨 Coloring books + tablet with downloads. For the 4-hour train ride.
🧥 1 warm layer per person. Prague evenings can drop to 18 °C; tram AC is cold.
👟 Comfortable walking shoes with grip. Cobblestones + potential rain = slippery.
