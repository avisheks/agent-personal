### Glossary of Terms Used

#### Valuation Metrics

**P/E (Price-to-Earnings Ratio)** -- What the market pays per dollar of earnings. Higher = more expensive relative to current profits.

**Forward P/E** -- P/E using next year's estimated earnings. Lower than trailing P/E when earnings are expected to grow.

**EV (Enterprise Value)** -- Total company value including debt, minus cash. What an acquirer would pay.

**EV/EBITDA** -- Enterprise value per dollar of operating cash earnings. Lower = cheaper. Useful for comparing companies with different debt levels.

**EV/Sales** -- Enterprise value per dollar of revenue. Used for high-growth or not-yet-profitable companies.

**P/S (Price-to-Sales)** -- Market cap per dollar of revenue. Simpler than EV/Sales (ignores debt/cash).

**FCF Yield** -- Free cash flow as percentage of market cap. Higher = more cash per dollar of market value.

#### Cash Flow Metrics

**OCF (Operating Cash Flow)** -- Cash generated from normal business operations.

**FCF (Free Cash Flow)** -- Cash available after maintaining/expanding assets. Could be returned to shareholders or used for growth.

**CapEx (Capital Expenditures)** -- Spending on physical assets. Capital-light if <10% of revenue, capital-heavy if >20%.

**FCF/NI (FCF-to-Net-Income Ratio)** -- Cash quality indicator. >100% means more cash than reported profit (high earnings quality). <80% may signal aggressive accounting.

#### Growth Metrics

**YoY (Year-over-Year)** -- Growth rate comparing same period to prior year. Removes seasonality.

**EPS (Earnings Per Share)** -- Net income allocated to each share.

#### Risk Metrics

**Annualized Volatility** -- Standard deviation of daily returns, annualized. <15% low, 15-25% medium, 25-40% high, >40% very high.

**Max Drawdown** -- Largest peak-to-trough decline. Measures worst-case historical loss.

**VaR 95%** -- Daily loss exceeded only 5% of trading days. A "bad day" threshold.

#### Valuation Models

**DCF (Discounted Cash Flow)** -- Projects future free cash flows and discounts to present value. Three scenarios: Bear, Base, Bull.
