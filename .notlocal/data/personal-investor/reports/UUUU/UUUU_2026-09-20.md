## 12. Things the Market May Be Missing

### 1. Medical Isotope Optionality is Potentially Transformative

**FACT:** Energy Fuels has developed capabilities to process Ra-226 into Ac-225, a critical isotope for targeted alpha therapy (TAT) in cancer treatment. Global Ac-225 supply is extremely limited.

**INFERENCE:** If TAT therapies gain broader FDA approvals (several in late-stage trials), demand for Ac-225 could increase 10-100x. Energy Fuels' nuclear processing infrastructure gives it unique ability to produce at scale. This business alone could be worth $500M-$2B at maturity.

**OPINION:** The market assigns near-zero value to this optionality because it is pre-revenue and 3-5 years from meaningful contribution. For long-term investors, this asymmetric option is free at current prices.

### 2. Domestic Uranium Supply Chain is a National Security Issue

**FACT:** The US imports approximately 95% of uranium for its reactors. Russia and allies control ~40% of global enrichment capacity.

**INFERENCE:** Escalating geopolitical tension would likely trigger emergency procurement for domestic uranium. Energy Fuels would be the primary beneficiary as the largest domestic producer.

**OPINION:** This "call option on geopolitical disruption" is underpriced because the market treats geopolitical risk as binary rather than recognizing the increasing probability of domestic preference policy.

### 3. REE Separation is Closer to Commercial Scale Than Perceived

**FACT:** Energy Fuels has been processing monazite sand and producing mixed rare earth carbonate, targeting full separation to individual rare earth oxides.
