## 3. Company Fundamentals

### Business Overview

Expedia Group operates a portfolio of online travel brands including Expedia, Hotels.com, Vrbo, Travelocity, Orbitz, and others. The company is one of the two dominant online travel agencies (OTAs) globally alongside Booking Holdings. **[INFERENCE]**

### Revenue Streams

1. **Lodging (majority ~65-70%):** Hotel and vacation rental bookings (Expedia, Hotels.com, Vrbo). **[INFERENCE]**
2. **Advertising and media (~10-15%):** Travel advertising solutions for hotels and destinations. **[INFERENCE]**
3. **Air, car, and other travel (~15-20%):** Flight bookings, car rentals, activities, and packages. **[INFERENCE]**

### Competitive Moats

- **Scale and brand portfolio:** Multiple well-known brands capture different travel segments. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Data advantage:** Billions of transactions provide pricing optimization and personalization capabilities. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Supply relationships:** Direct integrations with hundreds of thousands of hotels and properties globally. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Network effects:** More travelers attract more suppliers; more supply attracts more travelers. **[INFERENCE]**

## 11. Contrarian Analysis

### The Bull Case

1. **FCF yield of 9.8% is absurdly cheap.** EXPE generates $3.1B in FCF on a $31.7B market cap. Even with zero growth, this is a compelling return through buybacks alone. **[INFERENCE]**
2. **Q2 2026 may mark an inflection.** 14.0% revenue growth and $7.16 EPS suggest operational improvements or market share gains are accelerating. **[FACT/INFERENCE]**
3. **AI-driven efficiency.** Travel platforms are early adopters of AI for customer service, personalization, and pricing optimization, which could drive margin expansion. **[INFERENCE]**
4. **Share buybacks compound value.** With $3.1B+ annual FCF and relatively low reinvestment needs, aggressive buybacks could drive 5-8% annual EPS growth even with flat revenue. **[INFERENCE]**

### The Bear Case

1. **Travel is cyclical.** A recession would hit travel demand directly, as discretionary travel spending is among the first expenses consumers cut. **[INFERENCE]**
2. **Google competition.** Google's expanding travel search capabilities could disintermediate OTAs over time. **[INFERENCE]**
3. **Single-digit revenue growth is uninspiring.** 7.6% growth for a $31.7B company does not attract growth investors. **[INFERENCE]**
4. **Vrbo turnaround uncertainty.** Vrbo has underperformed Airbnb in the vacation rental space; a failed turnaround drags on the portfolio. **[INFERENCE]**

### Contrarian Verdict

EXPE is a classic value-with-a-catalyst opportunity. The FCF yield alone provides significant downside protection, while the Q2 2026 acceleration and Golden Cross provide the momentum catalyst that could trigger re-rating. **[INFERENCE]**
