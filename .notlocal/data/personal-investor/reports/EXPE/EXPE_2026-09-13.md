### FCF-to-Net-Income Ratio: 241%

The FCF/NI ratio of $3.11B / $1.29B = 241% is unusually high and demands explanation. **FACT**

**Likely drivers:** **INFERENCE**
1. **Merchant model working capital:** EXPE collects payment from travelers at booking but pays hotels at or after check-in. This timing benefit inflates operating cash flow relative to revenue recognition.
2. **Depreciation/amortization exceeding CapEx:** If D&A significantly exceeds CapEx ($770M), the difference adds to cash flow without appearing in earnings.
3. **Deferred revenue dynamics:** Pre-paid bookings for future travel create a positive working capital float.

**Assessment:** The high FCF/NI ratio is partially structural (merchant model) and partially reflects real cash generation superiority over GAAP earnings. However, the working capital benefit could reverse in a downturn (cancellations/refunds) -- as demonstrated in 2020 when FCF turned deeply negative at -$4.63B. **INFERENCE**
