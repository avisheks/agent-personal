## 11. Contrarian Analysis

### 11.1 The Bull Case That Bears Are Missing

1. **Cash Fortress:** ~$2.9B in net cash provides 4+ years of runway. Unlike many speculative tech companies, IonQ will not die from cash starvation in the near term. This buys time for quantum computing to mature. [INFERENCE]

2. **Government Backing:** Quantum computing is a strategic national priority. IonQ benefits from DARPA, DOE, and DOD contracts that provide a revenue floor and validation. Government customers are sticky. [INFERENCE]
