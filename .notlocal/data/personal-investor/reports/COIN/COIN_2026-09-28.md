## 3. Company Fundamentals

### Business Overview

Coinbase is the largest regulated cryptocurrency exchange in the United States and a key infrastructure provider for the crypto economy. **[INFERENCE]** The company has evolved from a pure trading platform to a broader crypto financial services provider.

### Revenue Streams

1. **Transaction Revenue (~50-60%):** Trading fees from retail and institutional crypto trading. Highly cyclical. **[INFERENCE]**
2. **Subscription & Services (~30-40%):** Staking rewards, custody fees, blockchain rewards (Base L2), interest income on USDC. Growing and more recurring. **[INFERENCE]**
3. **Other (~5-10%):** Venture investments, corporate revenue. **[INFERENCE]**

### Competitive Moats

- **Regulatory moat:** Only major crypto exchange with broad US regulatory compliance. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Brand trust:** Brand recognition as the "safe" crypto on-ramp for retail and institutional investors. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Base L2 network:** Coinbase's Layer 2 blockchain generates transaction fees and positions COIN in blockchain infrastructure. **[INFERENCE]**
- **USDC partnership:** Partnership with Circle on USDC stablecoin generates interest income. **[INFERENCE]**
