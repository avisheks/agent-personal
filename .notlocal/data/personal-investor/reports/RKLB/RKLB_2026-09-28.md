## 5. Historical Sentiment (5Y)

**2021 — SPAC Euphoria and Space Hype**
Rocket Lab went public via SPAC merger in August 2021, riding the wave of space enthusiasm. Retail sentiment was overwhelmingly bullish, fueled by comparisons to SpaceX and the narrative of a "second space age." The stock initially soared before pulling back sharply as SPAC enthusiasm faded. [INFERENCE]

**2022 — Post-SPAC Hangover**
Sentiment turned deeply negative as the stock cratered alongside other high-growth, unprofitable names in the 2022 rate-hike cycle. RKLB fell over 80% from its SPAC-era highs. Bears dominated the narrative, questioning whether the company could ever achieve profitability. [INFERENCE]

**2023 — Stabilization and Quiet Rebuilding**
Sentiment was mixed-to-neutral. Revenue growth decelerated (+7-35% YoY quarters), but Electron launch cadence improved and Space Systems revenue grew. The Neutron development program generated cautious optimism among space enthusiasts but skepticism from value investors. [INFERENCE]

**2024 — Breakout Year**
Sentiment swung dramatically bullish as revenue growth re-accelerated to +55-78% YoY. The stock surged from single digits to new highs. Neutron development milestones, expanding government contracts, and the broader AI/space investment theme drove retail and institutional enthusiasm. [INFERENCE]

**2025-2026 — Volatile Conviction**
The stock reached $150.23 before a severe 50%+ correction. Sentiment is currently mixed: long-term bulls view the pullback as a buying opportunity, while bears point to extreme valuation, persistent losses, and the death cross as warning signs. ### Bull Counter-Arguments (Against the Bearish Technical Signal)

1. **Death crosses in high-growth names are unreliable.** RKLB's historical max drawdown of 97.65% means the stock has survived far worse. Death crosses in 2022 preceded eventual multi-hundred-percent rallies. [FACT/INFERENCE]

2. **Revenue acceleration in H1 2026 is being ignored.** Q1 2026 (+63% YoY) and Q2 2026 (+62% YoY) show growth reaccelerating despite the stock price decline. The market may be mispricing the fundamental trajectory. [FACT/OPINION]

3. **Space sector is structurally undervalued.** Global space economy projections ($1T+ by 2040) suggest the TAM is large enough to justify aggressive multiples for the few credible participants. [INFERENCE]

### Bear Counter-Arguments (Against the Growth Narrative)

1. **$47B market cap on <$1B trailing revenue is historically extreme.** Even best-in-class SaaS companies rarely sustained 50x+ revenue multiples. RKLB has hardware economics, not software margins. [FACT/OPINION]

2. **Neutron is not yet operational.** The entire bull case depends on a rocket that has not yet flown. Development rockets routinely experience multi-year delays and cost overruns. [FACT]

3. **SpaceX Starship changes the competitive landscape.** If Starship achieves routine operations, it could commoditize launch services and undercut RKLB's pricing power across all payload classes. [INFERENCE]

<a id="12-things-market-may-be-missing"></a>
## 12. Things Market May Be Missing

### Potentially Underappreciated Factors

1. **Space Systems is the hidden gem.** Most narrative focuses on launch, but Space Systems (satellite manufacturing, components) may be the higher-margin, more scalable segment. The Q4 revenue spikes likely reflect large Space Systems contract deliveries. [INFERENCE]

2. **Government/defense optionality is not priced in.** As geopolitical competition in space intensifies (US-China rivalry), RKLB's domestic launch capability has strategic national security value. This could unlock non-commercial revenue streams with different pricing dynamics. [INFERENCE]

3. **The Q4 seasonality pattern is an asset, not a bug.** Consistent Q4 revenue spikes (Q4 2021: $62M, Q4 2022: $211M, Q4 2023: $245M, Q4 2024: $436M, Q4 2025: $602M) show ~50-100% sequential growth every Q4. If Q4 2026 follows this pattern, it could be $500M+ in a single quarter. [FACT/FORECAST]

4. **The quiet sentiment environment could be coiling for a move.** With only 8 posts in 6 months, RKLB is under-followed relative to its market cap. Any catalyst could generate outsized price moves due to low narrative positioning. [INFERENCE]

5. **Electron's competitive moat is strengthening.** With 50+ successful launches, Electron has an operational track record that new entrants will take years to replicate. This creates a durable, if smaller, revenue base regardless of Neutron's outcome. [FACT/INFERENCE]

### Potentially Overappreciated Factors

1. **SpaceX comparison is aspirational, not factual.** SpaceX has 10x+ the revenue, profitability, and Starlink as a captive customer. The analogy flatters RKLB's valuation beyond what fundamentals support. [OPINION]

2. **Hardware margins are structurally lower than the market assumes.** Unlike software, rockets require physical manufacturing, testing, and per-unit material costs. Gross margins will likely plateau below SaaS-like levels. [INFERENCE]
