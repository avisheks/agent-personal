## 12. Things the Market May Be Missing

**1. The Space Systems Flywheel is underappreciated** [OPINION]
Rocket Lab's vertical integration — building solar panels, avionics, reaction wheels, star trackers, separation systems in-house — means every satellite constellation deployment that uses RKLB components generates recurring revenue regardless of who launches. This is a platform business embedded in the broader space ecosystem, not just a launch company. The market tends to value it only as a launch company.

**2. Backlog quality and duration** [INFERENCE]
Government contracts (DoD, NRO, NASA) are multi-year, high-certainty bookings. Q4 consistently shows a massive revenue step-up (Q4 2022: $211M, Q4 2023: $245M, Q4 2024: $436M, Q4 2025: $602M).
