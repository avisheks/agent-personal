## 12. Things Market May Be Missing {#things-market-may-be-missing}

**1. FCF Yield Is Actually Attractive [INFERENCE]**

At $57.14 with a $1.965B market cap, trailing FCF of ~$36M represents a 1.8% FCF yield. However, if the Q2 2026 FCF of $159M is even partially sustainable (say $80M annualized), the FCF yield jumps to 4.1%. For a growth company, this is highly unusual and potentially undervalued.
