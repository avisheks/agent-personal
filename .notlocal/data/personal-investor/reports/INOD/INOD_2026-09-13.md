The consensus bull case is straightforward: AI is transforming every industry, AI models need training data, Innodata provides that data, therefore Innodata grows with AI. This "picks and shovels" narrative has driven the stock from penny-stock territory to a $1.9B market cap. **INFERENCE**

**1. The Appen Precedent Is Ominous** **INFERENCE**
- Appen Limited was the original "AI data" stock -- surged as tech giants outsourced labeling work, then collapsed from ~$40 AUD to ~$1-2 AUD as key customers brought annotation in-house.
- Innodata faces the identical structural risk: its largest customers have the resources and motivation to vertically integrate.

**2. Synthetic Data May Displace Human-Curated Data** **INFERENCE**
- Advances in synthetic data generation threaten to reduce demand for human-curated datasets.
- Major tech companies are investing heavily in synthetic data pipelines.
- If synthetic data quality reaches parity for most use cases, Innodata's core value proposition weakens.

**3. Revenue Visibility Is Poor** **INFERENCE**
- Project-based revenue tends to be lumpy and unpredictable.
- The absence of reported revenue figures from Q3 2024 onward is a red flag.

**4. Customer Concentration Is Likely Extreme** **INFERENCE**
- For a ~$318M company serving the AI data market, 2-3 hyperscaler customers likely represent 50%+ of revenue.
- Loss of a single major customer could halve revenue overnight.

**5. The 99.4% Max Drawdown Is Not Ancient History** **FACT/INFERENCE**

- Small-cap services companies with customer concentration and cyclical end markets can reprice violently.
