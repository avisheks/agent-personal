## 3. Company Fundamentals

**Company Profile:**

eBay Inc. (NASDAQ: EBAY) operates a global online marketplace connecting buyers and sellers. The platform focuses increasingly on specialty categories -- collectibles, trading cards, refurbished electronics, luxury goods, and auto parts -- rather than competing head-to-head with Amazon in general merchandise. Revenue comes primarily from marketplace fees (take rate on GMV) and advertising. eBay completed the sale of its classified ads business to Adevinta in 2021 and has since focused on core marketplace optimization.

3. **AI Benefits Accrue to Platforms with Catalog Depth** [INFERENCE] eBay has billions of historical listings. AI-powered search, pricing recommendations, and listing assistance could significantly improve the platform. eBay's unstructured catalog is uniquely positioned to benefit from LLM-powered search.

4. **Currency and Tariff Fears May Be Overstated** [INFERENCE] The majority of GMV is domestic-to-domestic. Tariff impacts primarily affect new merchandise sellers, not the collectibles/used goods categories where eBay is strongest.

5. **The Stock Has Quietly Tripled from 2022 Lows** [INFERENCE] From ~$37 in late 2022 to $108 today, confirmed by the 30-month Golden Cross, suggesting institutional accumulation.
