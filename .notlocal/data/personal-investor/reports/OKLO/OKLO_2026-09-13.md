## 9. Valuation vs Expectations {#valuation}

### 9a. Reverse DCF -- What the Market Implies

The current enterprise value of $4.16B implies extraordinary future revenue and profitability. Working backward from EV to determine what the market is pricing in:

**Assumptions for Reverse DCF:**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Current EV | $4.16B |
| Discount Rate (WACC) | 12% |
| Terminal Growth Rate | 3% |
| Projection Period | 10 years |
| Tax Rate | 21% |

**Implied Year-10 Revenue at Various Terminal Margins:**

| Terminal Net Margin | Implied Year-10 Revenue | Implied Revenue CAGR (from $1.21M) | Plausibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10% | ~$4.6B | ~145% CAGR | Extremely unlikely |
| 15% | ~$3.1B | ~135% CAGR | Extremely unlikely |
| 20% | ~$2.3B | ~128% CAGR | Extremely unlikely |
| 25% | ~$1.8B | ~122% CAGR | Very unlikely |
| 30% | ~$1.5B | ~118% CAGR | Very unlikely |

**Methodology:** EV = Sum of discounted FCFs + Terminal Value. Terminal Value = (Year-10 FCF x (1+g)) / (WACC - g). FCF approximated as Revenue x Net Margin x (1 - Tax Rate) + D&A - CapEx, simplified to Revenue x Terminal Margin for this analysis. [INFERENCE]

**Interpretation:** Even at an aggressive 30% terminal net margin (comparable to the most profitable utility companies), the market is implying Oklo will reach ~$1.5B in annual revenue within 10 years -- a compound growth rate of ~118% annually from the current $1.21M quarterly run rate. For context, this would make Oklo roughly the size of a mid-cap utility, built from essentially zero, in an industry where reactor construction typically takes 5-10 years per unit. [INFERENCE]

**The math is stark:** The current valuation requires Oklo to become a multi-billion dollar revenue company with best-in-class margins within a decade. This is not impossible but represents an extremely low-probability outcome that the market is pricing with high confidence. [OPINION]

## 12. Things the Market May Be Missing {#market-missing}

### Potentially Underappreciated by Bulls

1. **HALEU supply chain risk.** Oklo's fast reactors require High-Assay Low-Enriched Uranium, which is currently only produced commercially by Russia (via Tenex). The DOE's domestic HALEU program is behind schedule. Without HALEU, Oklo cannot fuel its reactors regardless of NRC approval. This supply chain dependency is rarely discussed in bull narratives. [INFERENCE]

2. **Construction execution risk.** Even after NRC approval and fuel procurement, Oklo must actually build reactors on time and on budget. The history of nuclear construction in the U.S. is one of massive cost overruns and multi-year delays (see: Vogtle Units 3&4, which went from $14B to $35B). Oklo's "compact" design may mitigate this, but it is unproven. [FACT/INFERENCE]

3. **Insurance and liability.** Commercial nuclear reactors require massive insurance coverage. The Price-Anderson Act covers conventional reactors, but its applicability to advanced reactors is evolving. Insurance costs could be a significant and underappreciated expense. [INFERENCE]

4. **Revenue recognition question.** The $1.21M in Q2 2026 revenue -- what is it? If it is consulting revenue, DOE contract work, or similar, it may not indicate progress toward the core reactor business. The nature of first revenue matters enormously for the narrative. [INFERENCE]

### Potentially Underappreciated by Bears

1. **Government tailwinds are intensifying.** Bipartisan support for nuclear energy is at its highest level since the 1960s. The ADVANCE Act, DOE loan guarantees, and defense applications create multiple revenue paths that do not depend solely on commercial data center contracts. [INFERENCE]

2. **The "picks and shovels" optionality.** Oklo's HALEU recycling capability could become a standalone business serving the entire advanced nuclear industry, not just its own reactors. This optionality is not priced into most bear models. [INFERENCE]

3. **Defense applications.** Project Pele and other DoD microreactor programs could provide early revenue and regulatory fast-tracking that commercial applications cannot. Military use cases have different (potentially faster) regulatory pathways. [INFERENCE]

4. **Talent acquisition advantage.** The combination of Altman's network, a well-capitalized balance sheet, and mission-driven work in clean energy gives Oklo a recruiting advantage for top nuclear engineers and physicists. Human capital is the binding constraint in advanced nuclear. [INFERENCE]
