## 11. Contrarian Analysis

[Back to Top](#top)

### Contrarian Bull Case

1. **78.2% Decline Has Reset Expectations:** Most of the speculative premium is gone. At $38.04 (vs. $174.14 high), the stock is priced for failure, meaning any positive surprise has outsized upside. [OPINION]

2. **Net Cash Provides Runway:** With ~$2.5B net cash, Oklo has approximately 5-6 years of runway at current burn rates (~$50-65M/quarter OCF). This is significantly longer than most pre-revenue companies. [INFERENCE]

3. **Nuclear Narrative Is Early, Not Dead:** The AI data center power demand thesis is only strengthening. Policy support for nuclear (ADVANCE Act, DOE funding programs) creates a favorable backdrop. [INFERENCE]

4. **Near 52-Week Low = Maximum Pessimism:** The technical system's "Good entry" signal despite the Death Cross reflects the contrarian logic that buying near maximum pessimism produces the best long-term returns. [FACT, OPINION]

### Contrarian Bear Case

1. **Licensing Is Not Guaranteed:** Oklo's initial Combined License Application was rejected by the NRC. Resubmission and approval could take 3-5+ years with no certainty of success. [FACT]

2. **Nuclear Construction History:** The nuclear industry's track record of cost overruns and schedule delays (see: Vogtle, NuScale) should give investors pause. Oklo's compact design may not avoid these issues. [INFERENCE]

3. **Dilution Inevitable:** Even with $2.5B cash, building nuclear reactors will require billions more. Equity issuance at depressed prices is highly dilutive to current shareholders. [INFERENCE]
