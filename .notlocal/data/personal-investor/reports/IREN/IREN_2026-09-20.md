## 12. Things the Market May Be Missing

### Potentially Underappreciated Factors

1. **Energy Arbitrage Expertise:** IREN's Bitcoin mining operations required mastering energy procurement, power infrastructure, and cooling at scale -- exactly the competencies needed for AI data centers. The market may underestimate how transferable these skills are. **[OPINION]**

2. **Regulatory Tailwind:** As governments worldwide push for domestic AI compute capacity, companies with operational data center infrastructure may benefit from subsidies, tax credits, or preferential permitting. IREN's existing facilities could be grandfathered into favorable regulatory frameworks. **[INFERENCE]**

3. **Depreciation Masking Cash Generation:** The massive CapEx creates enormous depreciation charges that depress reported earnings. Operating cash flow ($214.0M in Q4 2025) tells a different story than net income (-$155.4M). As CapEx normalizes, the earnings-to-OCF gap will narrow favorably. **[INFERENCE]**

4. **Strategic Acquisition Target:** A company with operational data center infrastructure, power agreements, and cooling expertise is extremely attractive to hyperscalers, private equity, or sovereign wealth funds looking to acquire rather than build AI infrastructure. **[OPINION]**

5. **Bitcoin Mining Floor Value:** Even in a worst-case AI pivot failure, IREN's Bitcoin mining operations have standalone value. The liquidation floor for the business is likely higher than the market assumes in bear scenarios. **[INFERENCE]**

### Potentially Overappreciated Factors

1. **Revenue Growth Rate:** The deceleration from 167.7% to 41.1% YoY is significant. If growth continues to decelerate at this rate, revenue growth could approach single digits within 2-3 years, undermining the growth premium. **[INFERENCE]**

2. **AI Infrastructure Demand Durability:** Current AI CapEx is driven by a training compute race that may reach diminishing returns. If inference workloads require less compute than projected, data center demand could disappoint. **[OPINION]**

3. **Management Track Record:** The team has successfully grown a Bitcoin mining operation, but enterprise data center operations require entirely different customer relationships, SLAs, and operational discipline. The market may be overweighting past success in a different domain. **[OPINION]**
