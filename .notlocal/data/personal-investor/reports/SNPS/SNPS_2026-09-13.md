### Quality Gate Verdict: HOLD — High-Conviction Compounder at Fair-to-Full Valuation

Synopsys is the co-leader of the EDA duopoly alongside Cadence (CDNS), commanding one of the widest competitive moats in technology. The business has compounded EPS at ~18% annualized over 15 years, growing from $1.15 (FY2009) to $14.51 (FY2024). [FACT: derived from SEC filings in research packet]

## 12. Things the Market May Be Missing

### 1. The Combined Entity's Cash Generation Power

Q3 FY2026 FCF of $2.14B is an extraordinary quarterly number. While it may include timing benefits (prepaid license collections, working capital), even a normalized $5-6B annual FCF for the combined entity would represent a transformational improvement over legacy Synopsys's ~$1.4B. ### 2. The EDA Pricing Cycle

EDA contracts are typically 3-5 year agreements. As existing contracts roll to post-AI-complexity pricing, average contract values should increase materially. This pricing tailwind is not yet visible in reported numbers but will compound over the next 2-3 years. [FORECAST]

### 3. AI as a Product Catalyst, Not Just a Customer Catalyst

Synopsys has been embedding AI/ML into its EDA tools (Synopsys.ai) to accelerate design closure, reduce time-to-market, and improve chip performance. This creates a usage-based pricing opportunity on top of traditional license revenue — a business model evolution the market may not fully appreciate. [OPINION]

### 4. Ansys Cross-Sell into Semiconductor Customers

Synopsys has deep relationships with every major semiconductor company. Ansys's simulation tools (thermal, electromagnetic, structural) are increasingly critical for advanced chip packaging (chiplets, 3D stacking). Synopsys can bundle and cross-sell Ansys products into its existing customer base at minimal incremental sales cost. [INFERENCE]

### 5. Defensive Characteristics Underappreciated

EDA spending is non-discretionary for chip companies. Even in semiconductor downturns, EDA budgets are among the last to be cut because design teams continue working on next-generation chips during production slowdowns. This counter-cyclical resilience is evidenced by SNPS's COVID-era performance (EPS grew from $3.45 to $4.27 during FY2019-FY2020). [FACT/INFERENCE]

### 6. The Duopoly Strengthens Over Time

Each new technology node increases the complexity and cost of EDA tools, raising barriers to entry. No new EDA competitor has gained meaningful market share in over 15 years. The Ansys deal adds a third "pillar" (simulation) that further entrenches Synopsys's position. [OPINION]
