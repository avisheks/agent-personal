## 9. Valuation vs Expectations

### (a) Reverse DCF: What Does $364 Imply?

**Assumptions:** 10% discount rate (WACC), 3% terminal growth rate, 25x terminal FCF multiple.

To justify a $1.44T market cap at a 10% discount rate, Tesla would need to generate approximately $55-65B in annual free cash flow by 2033 (7 years out). [INFERENCE] This compares to $6.22B in FY2025 FCF -- implying roughly 9-10x growth in free cash flow over 7 years, or ~35% CAGR. [INFERENCE]

This would make Tesla one of the top 3-5 FCF generators globally, alongside Apple, Microsoft, and Saudi Aramco. [INFERENCE] Achieving this requires not just revenue growth but a dramatic shift in margin structure -- from the current ~4% net margin to 20%+, which is only plausible if high-margin software/AI revenue becomes dominant. [INFERENCE]

**Reverse DCF Verdict:** The current price implies Tesla successfully transitions from an auto manufacturer to a high-margin technology platform. The probability of this outcome is non-trivial but far from certain. [OPINION]

### (b) Forward DCF: Bear / Base / Bull

**Bear Case ($60-$140/share):**
- Tesla remains primarily an auto company [FORECAST]
- Revenue grows modestly to $120-130B by 2030 [FORECAST]
- Net margins stabilize at 5-7% (auto industry norm) [FORECAST]
- FSD remains Level 2+ (driver assistance, not autonomy) [FORECAST]
- Optimus does not reach commercial scale [FORECAST]
- Fair value: $60-$140/share (P/E 15-25x on $4-6 normalized EPS) [FORECAST]
- Downside from current: -62% to -84% [INFERENCE]

**Base Case ($200-$350/share):**
- Auto revenue grows to $130-150B by 2030 [FORECAST]
- Energy storage becomes a $25-30B business [FORECAST]
- FSD generates meaningful licensing/subscription revenue ($5-10B) [FORECAST]
- Net margins recover to 10-12% [FORECAST]
- Robotaxi launches but at limited scale [FORECAST]
- Fair value: $200-$350/share (P/E 30-40x on $6-9 EPS) [FORECAST]
- Return from current: -4% to -45% [INFERENCE]

**Bull Case ($400-$600+/share):**
- Robotaxi network launches at scale, generating $20-30B in high-margin revenue [FORECAST]
