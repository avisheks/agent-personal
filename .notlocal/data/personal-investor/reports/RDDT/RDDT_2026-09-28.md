## 3. Company Fundamentals

### Business Overview

Reddit operates the world's largest community-driven discussion platform, monetized primarily through advertising and AI data licensing. **[FACT]** The platform hosts over 100,000 active communities (subreddits) covering virtually every topic. **[INFERENCE]**

### Revenue Streams

1. **Advertising (estimated 70-80% of revenue):** Display, video, and performance advertising served across subreddit feeds and search results. **[INFERENCE]**
2. **Data Licensing (estimated 15-25% of revenue):** Licensing Reddit's conversational data corpus to AI companies for model training. Key deals include Google and OpenAI. **[INFERENCE]**
3. **Premium / Other (estimated <5%):** Reddit Premium subscriptions and virtual goods. **[INFERENCE]**

### Competitive Moats

- **Network effects:** Self-reinforcing community engagement with no close substitutes. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Data moat:** Uniquely valuable corpus of authentic, topic-organized human discussion for AI training. **[INFERENCE]**

## 11. Contrarian Analysis

### The Bull Case the Market Is Ignoring

1. **Forward P/E is absurdly low for growth:** 15.5x forward P/E on 60%+ revenue growth is a PEG of 0.25. The average S&P 500 company trades at 18-20x forward P/E on 5-10% growth. **[INFERENCE]**
2. **The death cross is a false signal:** For companies with accelerating fundamentals, death crosses frequently mark capitulation lows. **[INFERENCE]**
3. **FCF yield is approaching value territory:** At 2.4% FCF yield on a 60%+ grower, Reddit's FCF yield will likely exceed 4-5% within 12-18 months. **[INFERENCE]**
4. **The SBC fear is overdone:** Even at worst-case SBC ($600M), Reddit would be approximately breakeven on an adjusted basis. **[INFERENCE]**

### The Bear Case the Market May Be Right About

1. **Revenue growth will decelerate faster than expected:** AI data licensing deals may be non-recurring or face pricing pressure. **[INFERENCE]**
2. **Ad market competition is brutal:** Reddit's ad platform is less sophisticated than Meta's or Google's. **[INFERENCE]**
3. **The stock traded to $259 on momentum, not fundamentals:** Current price may still embed momentum premium. **[INFERENCE]**
4. **Volatility destroys compounding:** At 79.3% annualized volatility, geometric returns are significantly lower than arithmetic returns. **[INFERENCE]**

### Contrarian Verdict

Weight of evidence leans slightly toward the contrarian bull case. Position sizing should be conservative (1-2% of portfolio max) given the volatility. **[INFERENCE]**
