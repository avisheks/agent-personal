### 2.2 The SBC Problem

**Why SBC matters for RDDT:**

Reddit went public via IPO in March 2024. The Q1 2024 EPS of -$8.19 on only $243M revenue is a massive red flag -- it implies approximately $575M in net losses on a quarter with only $243M in revenue. **[FACT]** This almost certainly includes an enormous one-time SBC charge related to the IPO (vesting acceleration, RSU settlement, etc.). **[INFERENCE]**

**Estimating the SBC impact:**

1. **Q1 2024 anomaly:** Revenue of $243M but net loss of -$575M implies ~$818M in total costs+expenses. Even with operating losses typical of pre-profit Reddit, this suggests $400-500M in non-cash charges (primarily SBC). **[INFERENCE]**
2. **FCF vs. NI spread:** In FY2025, FCF ($684M) exceeds NI ($530M) by $155M. Since SBC is added back in operating cash flow, this $155M gap provides a floor estimate. However, the true SBC is likely much higher because CapEx is minimal ($6.7M) and other non-cash items could offset. **[INFERENCE]**
3. **Industry comparison:** For context, Meta's SBC was ~10-12% of revenue in its early public years; Snap's SBC exceeded 30% of revenue post-IPO. If Reddit's SBC is 15-25% of its $2.2B revenue, that implies $330-550M in annual SBC. **[INFERENCE]**
4. **Adjusted EPS estimate:** If SBC is ~$400M (midpoint estimate), after-tax impact (~$320M at 20% tax rate) would reduce NI from $530M to ~$210M, or ~$1.04 EPS -- roughly 60% lower than GAAP. **[INFERENCE]**

### 2.3 Operating Leverage Trajectory

The profitability inflection is the centerpiece of the RDDT story. Reddit went from burning cash to generating substantial profits in just four quarters, demonstrating textbook operating leverage in a platform business.

**The inflection in numbers:**

**What this shows:** Revenue grew 131% from Q3 2024 to Q2 2026 ($348M to $805M), but net income grew 746% ($29.9M to $252.8M). **[FACT]** For every incremental dollar of revenue, an increasing share drops to the bottom line. NI margin expanded from 8.6% to 31.4% in just seven quarters. **[FACT]**

**Why it matters:** Platform businesses like Reddit have high fixed costs (engineering, infrastructure, content moderation) but near-zero marginal costs per additional user or ad impression. Once revenue clears the fixed cost base, incremental margins are extremely high -- potentially 60-80%. **[INFERENCE]** Reddit appears to have crossed this threshold in mid-2024, and each subsequent quarter shows the leverage compounding.

**Forward implication:** If revenue grows another 50% from H1 2026 levels (plausible given the trajectory), and incremental margins remain at 40-50%, FY2027 net income could reach $800M-$1.2B, implying EPS of $4.00-$6.00. **[INFERENCE]** The current price of $152.10 would then represent just 25-38x earnings -- reasonable for a 50%+ grower.

### Business Model

Reddit generates revenue through three primary channels:

1. **Advertising (Primary):** Promoted posts, display ads, and video ads sold across Reddit's highly engaged, interest-organized communities. Advertisers value Reddit's intent-rich audiences and authentic engagement. **[FACT]**
2. **Data Licensing (High Growth):** Licensing Reddit's massive corpus of human-generated conversational data to AI companies training large language models. Major deals with Google, OpenAI, and others have made this a significant revenue contributor. **[FACT/INFERENCE]**
3. **Reddit Premium & Awards:** Subscription revenue from Reddit Premium and virtual goods. This is a smaller contributor. **[FACT]**

### Competitive Moat

Reddit possesses several durable competitive advantages:

- **Network effects:** Millions of self-organizing communities create content that attracts users, which attracts more content creators. This flywheel is 20 years deep. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Data moat:** Reddit's corpus of authentic, topic-organized human discussion is uniquely valuable for AI training -- no other platform offers comparable breadth and depth of genuine human conversation. **[INFERENCE]**
- **SEO dominance:** Reddit pages rank highly in Google search results for nearly every topic, providing a massive free user acquisition channel. Google's own AI products increasingly cite Reddit discussions. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Low CapEx model:** With CapEx/Revenue below 0.3%, Reddit converts nearly all operating cash flow to free cash flow. **[FACT]**

Reddit is the only major platform with a massive corpus of authentic, topic-organized, human-authored conversational data that is actively being licensed for AI training. **[INFERENCE]** Unlike web scraping (which is legally contested) or synthetic data (which degrades model quality), Reddit's data is consensually licensed, high-quality, and continuously refreshed by 50M+ daily active users. **[INFERENCE]** As AI model generations increase in size and sophistication, demand for Reddit's data should increase, not decrease.
