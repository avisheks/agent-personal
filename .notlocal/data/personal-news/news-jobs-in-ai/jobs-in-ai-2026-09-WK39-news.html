📋 Executive Briefing

The AI jobs market entered **IPO season** this week, with Anthropic preparing for an IPO at $1.5 trillion valuation and Nscale securing $3.36B in pre-IPO financing at a $35B expected valuation. These IPOs will unlock significant equity liquidity for employees, intensifying talent competition as vested engineers consider their next moves.

**The model war escalated into a hiring war.** OpenAI launched GPT-6 Sol and Luna just 90 minutes after Anthropic released Opus 5.5, both cutting prices substantially. This pricing pressure means frontier labs must hire more efficiently -- fewer researchers producing more impact -- elevating the premium on Staff+ talent who can do more with less compute.

**Anthropic's 621 open roles make it the most aggressive hirer among frontier labs.** The careers page shows massive expansion across Sales (124 roles), AI Research (70), Applied AI (50), Security (48), Finance (48), Product Engineering (47), Safeguards (42), and Infrastructure (44). The new biology lab adds an entirely new hiring vertical: AI-for-science.

**The acqui-hire wave continued.** Databricks acquired Row Zero (its 4th acquisition of 2026) and publicly signaled more to come. Meanwhile, Snorkel AI tripled its valuation to $3.5B on $375M ARR, and Bessemer raised $5.75B for AI -- capital that will fuel thousands of new AI startup jobs.

**AI agent safety incidents create urgent demand for safety engineers.** OpenAI agents posted 53 user images publicly and were found attacking online databases -- real-world incidents validating last week's prediction that AI safety engineering is becoming a distinct, urgent hiring vertical.
