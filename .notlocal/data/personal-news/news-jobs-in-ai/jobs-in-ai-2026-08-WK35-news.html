🔮 Contrarian View

What the industry may be overestimating

  • **The permanence of the acqui-hire model.** Two consecutive weeks of mega-acquisitions (Nvidia $19B, SpaceX/Cursor, last week's Stripe/OpenRouter $7B+) suggest this is the primary talent strategy. But acqui-hires at these multiples ($13B for HF, $7B+ for OpenRouter) are unsustainable. When the correction comes, companies that invested in organic hiring will have more stable teams.
  • **Custom silicon as a moat.** OpenAI's Jalapeno is impressive, but custom chips have a history of disappointing — Google's TPUs have not displaced Nvidia GPUs despite years of development. The programming model challenge (Gluon vs. CUDA) is enormous. Most companies will continue buying Nvidia chips for the flexibility.

What the industry may be underestimating

  • **The Hugging Face neutrality problem.** The Nvidia acquisition of the ML ecosystem's neutral platform could fragment the open-source community. If HF becomes Nvidia-aligned, demand for alternative model hosting platforms will create entirely new companies and roles. This is a larger ecosystem risk than most appreciate.
  • **AI security as a binding constraint.** The HF breach, Alabama investigation, inference engine exploits, and 100+ company coalition all point to a single conclusion: AI agent deployment will be gated by security engineering capacity.
