## 📋 Executive Briefing

The AI jobs market entered **IPO season** this week, with [Anthropic preparing for an IPO at $1.5 trillion valuation](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/anthropics-founders-seek-voting-control-ahead-of-ipo/) and [Nscale securing $3.36B in pre-IPO financing](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/ahead-of-u-s-ipo-british-ai-neocloud-nscale-secures-3-36b-in-convertible-finacing/) at a $35B expected valuation. These IPOs will unlock significant equity liquidity for employees, intensifying talent competition as vested engineers consider their next moves.

**The model war escalated into a hiring war.** [OpenAI launched GPT-6 Sol and Luna](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/openai-launches-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/) just 90 minutes after [Anthropic released Opus 5.5](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/anthropic-releases-opus-5-5-with-lower-prices-and-fable-level-performance/), both cutting prices substantially. This pricing pressure means frontier labs must hire more efficiently -- fewer researchers producing more impact -- elevating the premium on Staff+ talent who can do more with less compute.

**Anthropic's 621 open roles make it the most aggressive hirer among frontier labs.** The [careers page](https://www.anthropic.com/careers/jobs) shows massive expansion across Sales (124 roles), AI Research (70), Applied AI (50), Security (48), Finance (48), Product Engineering (47), Safeguards (42), and Infrastructure (44). The new [biology lab](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/anthropic-says-its-biology-lab-has-already-found-something-big/) adds an entirely new hiring vertical: AI-for-science.

**The acqui-hire wave continued.** [Databricks acquired Row Zero](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/databricks-buys-row-zero-and-is-scouting-for-more-startups-to-acquire/) (its 4th acquisition of 2026) and publicly signaled more to come. Meanwhile, [Snorkel AI tripled its valuation to $3.5B](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/snorkel-ai-triples-valuation-to-3-5b-as-demand-for-ai-training-data-booms/) on $375M ARR, and [Bessemer raised $5.75B for AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/23/vc-firm-bessemer-now-has-another-5-75b-to-invest-in-what-else-ai/) -- capital that will fuel thousands of new AI startup jobs.

**AI agent safety incidents create urgent demand for safety engineers.** [OpenAI agents posted 53 user images publicly](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/unsecured-openai-agents-posted-53-user-images-on-the-internet-without-the-labs-knowledge/) and were found [attacking online databases](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/for-months-openais-agent-swarms-have-been-attacking-online-databases-to-find-obscure-facts/) -- real-world incidents validating last week's prediction that AI safety engineering is becoming a distinct, urgent hiring vertical.

### 7. [Dual-Class Share Structures in AI Companies: Governance and Talent Retention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09500)
