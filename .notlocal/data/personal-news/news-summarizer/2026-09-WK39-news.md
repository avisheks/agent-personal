In business, [Anthropic signed an $11.6B cloud deal with Akamai](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/anthropic-to-pay-akamai-11-6-billion-over-seven-years-in-cloud-deal/), [Anthropic's founders seek voting control ahead of IPO](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/anthropics-founders-seek-voting-control-ahead-of-ipo/), and [Meta's Muse is outpacing ChatGPT's early mobile launch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/21/metas-muse-is-outpacing-chatgpts-early-mobile-launch/).

### 2. Claude Discovers Novel CRISPR-Like Enzyme System
| Metric | Score |
|--------|-------|
| Strategic Importance | 10 |
| Technical Innovation | 10 |
| Practical Adoption | 8 |
| Business Impact | 9 |
| Confidence | High |

**Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system) | **Reading time:** 8 min | 🧪 Early prototype

[Anthropic's life sciences research group](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system) deployed ~950 Claude agents to analyze 210 million tokens of DNA sequence data over 21 hours. They discovered array-associated reverse transcriptases (ART) — a previously unknown enzyme system in bacteriophages featuring a reverse transcriptase, an associated protein, and CRISPR-like repeat arrays. [Feng Zhang (MIT)](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system) called it "an exciting example of how AI agents can contribute to biological discovery."

**Implications:** This is the first credible case of an AI autonomously discovering a novel biological system. Combined with [OpenAI solving 100+ open math problems](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/21/openai-forms-math-advisory-group-as-its-ai-resolves-more-than-100-open-problems/) and [Claude computing nine-loop amplitudes](https://www.anthropic.com/research/yes-claude-can-do-nine-loops), AI-as-researcher is transitioning from concept to demonstrated capability.

**Audience:** Life sciences leaders, research directors, CTOs evaluating AI for R&D
