## Quick Catchup

> **Quick Catchup (June 2026):** Gen-AI has restructured (not killed) online advertising. Google's AI Overviews reach 1B+ users with integrated ads; OpenAI launched ChatGPT ads (projected $2.5B/2026); Meta's Advantage+ automates campaigns at $201B scale [1][2][3].
> Key shift: "shortlist economy" — AI agents pre-filter 3-5 options before humans see them (Microsoft thesis). Main open problem: whether ad-supported AI answers can coexist with user trust (Perplexity reversed course Feb 2026) [4].
> Recent breakthrough: Agentic commerce protocols (Google Direct Offers, OpenAI+Stripe, Microsoft Copilot Checkout) enabling AI-mediated purchases within search/chat [2][5].

## State of the Art

### Current Best Approaches

- **AI-integrated search ads** — Google AI Overviews + ads (1B+ users; "links get MORE clicks than traditional listings") [1]
- **Keywordless campaign optimization** — Google AI Max uses Gemini to match intent without explicit keywords (14% more conversions) [1]
- **End-to-end campaign automation** — Meta Advantage+ (audience, creative, bid, placement all AI-driven) [3]
- **Gen-AI creative production** — Amazon AI images (+40% CTR), Google Veo/Imagen, Meta full image gen, TikTok Symphony video [1][3][6]
- **Agentic commerce** — AI agents completing purchases (OpenAI Operator, Microsoft Copilot Checkout, Google Direct Offers) [2][5]

### Recent Breakthroughs (2024-2026)

- **Google AI Overviews + Ads** (2025): Ads integrated into AI-generated summaries without reducing organic clicks [1]
- **OpenAI ChatGPT Ads** (Jan-Mar 2026): First ads in ChatGPT; projected $2.5B revenue 2026; ambition $100B by 2030 [2]
- **Google Direct Offers** (Jan 2026): Native checkout within AI search via Universal Commerce Protocol [1]

### Open Problems

- **Ad trust in AI answers**: Can ads coexist with "objective" AI synthesis? (Perplexity says no) [4]
- **Agentic commerce attribution**: When an AI agent buys on behalf of a user, who gets credit for the "conversion"?
- **Shortlist visibility**: How do brands ensure inclusion in AI-pre-filtered shortlists without traditional ad auctions?
- **Creative homogenization**: AI-generated ads at scale may converge on similar patterns, reducing differentiation

## Executive Summary

The digital advertising industry ($700B+ globally) is being restructured by gen-AI along five axes: keywords→intent, human creative→AI production, search→agentic commerce, tracking→contextual AI, and the emergence of the "shortlist economy." Revenue is GROWING (Google $403B, Meta $201B in 2025) but the mechanism is fundamentally changing [1][3].

**The killer framing:** "The ad industry isn't dying — it's being restructured. The question isn't whether AI replaces ads, but whether your brand is optimized for the AI shortlist or invisible to it."

## Five Structural Shifts

### 1. Keywords → Intent

Google AI Max explicitly uses **"keywordless technology"** — learns from landing pages and creative to match user intent without keyword lists. All platforms (Meta Advantage+, TikTok Smart+, Amazon) moving from "what words did they type" to "what are they trying to accomplish."

**Implication:** Keyword research as a discipline is declining. Campaign optimization shifts to creative quality, landing page relevance, and objective definition.

### 2. Human Creative → AI Production

| Company | Tool | Impact |
|---------|------|--------|
| Amazon | AI Image Generator | **40%+ CTR** vs white-background |
| Google | Veo (video) + Imagen (image) | In-platform creative generation |
| Meta | Full image gen + text variation | Automated A/B at scale |
| TikTok | Symphony (script → video) | AI avatar + dubbing |

**Implication:** Human role shifts from PRODUCTION to DIRECTION. Brands define parameters; AI generates at scale. Creative teams become "AI creative directors" not "producers."

### 3. Search Ads → Agentic Commerce

| Product | What Happens |
|---------|-------------|
| OpenAI Operator (Jan 2025) | AI agent navigates sites, completes purchases |
| ChatGPT + Stripe (Sep 2025) | In-conversation buying (Agentic Commerce Protocol) |
| Google Direct Offers (Jan 2026) | Native checkout within AI search |
| Microsoft Copilot Checkout (2026) | End-to-end AI-mediated commerce |

**Implication:** When AI agents buy on behalf of users, advertising shifts from "persuade the human" to "be recommended by the algorithm." Requires: structured data, real-time inventory, review signals, reliable fulfillment — not traditional brand advertising.

### 4. Third-Party Tracking → AI Contextual

Cookie deprecation + privacy (GDPR, ATT) → first-party data + LLM-based contextual understanding replaces user-level tracking. Performance Max and Advantage+ rely on conversion signals, not browsing history.

### 5. "Shortlist Economy" (Microsoft Thesis)

AI agents pre-filter to 3-5 product recommendations before humans see them. **Brands not in the AI shortlist never reach human consideration.**

**Requirements for shortlist inclusion:**
- Structured product data (schema.org, feeds)
- Real-time inventory accuracy
- Third-party review aggregation
- Reliable fulfillment signals
- Machine-readable pricing/availability

This is fundamentally different from traditional advertising (brand awareness, creative persuasion, keyword bidding).

## Agentic Commerce Deep Dive

The most significant structural disruption ahead:

```
Traditional commerce:         Agentic commerce:
Human → Search → Ads → Click → Browse → Buy    AI Agent → Evaluate → Buy

Old optimization:             New optimization:
- Bid on keywords             - Be in AI's training data
- Creative that catches eye   - Structured data AI can parse
- Landing page CRO            - Reliable inventory/fulfillment
- Retargeting                 - Review signals AI trusts
```

**Protocols emerging:**
- **Agentic Commerce Protocol** (OpenAI + Stripe): Standard for AI-mediated transactions
- **Universal Commerce Protocol** (Google): Native checkout within AI search
- **Unified Commerce Protocols** (Microsoft): Cross-platform transactability

## LLM-Based Behavior Simulators for Ads & Search

> **Added:** 2026-06-08 | **Source:** Agent4Rec (SIGIR 2024), Google SIGIR 2024, BASES (EMNLP 2024), Shop-r1 (2025), LLM-Augmented Digital Twin (2026)

### What It Is

Using persona-prompted LLMs to generate realistic shopper/advertiser interaction sequences (queries, clicks, bids, conversions) for **offline testing** — replacing expensive live A/B tests.

### Company Leaderboard

| Company | System | What It Simulates |
|---------|--------|------------------|
| **Google** | RecSim NG + YouTube Music (SIGIR 2024) | Preference elicitation policies without live experiments |
| **Amazon** | Synthetic query gen (WWW 2024) + MiniELM (ACL 2025) | Cold-start products + RL training for query rewriting |
| **Microsoft** | BASES (EMNLP 2024) | Large-scale web search behavior (bilingual) |
| **ByteDance** | LLM-Augmented Digital Twin (2026) | Four-twin architecture for platform policy evaluation |
| **Meta** | (No public papers) | Likely proprietary internal systems |

### Four Technical Approaches

1. **LLM-as-User**: Persona → queries/clicks/purchases (Agent4Rec: 1K agents, 349 citations)
2. **LLM-as-Advertiser**: Simulate bidding/budgets/creative (LBM, Synthetic Labs — emerging)
3. **Generative Environment**: LLMs model entire marketplace dynamics (Digital Twin)
4. **Hybrid**: LLM personas + classical simulation (CXSimulator, Google RecSim NG)

### Key Applications

- Offline policy evaluation (Google YouTube Music — production deployed)
- A/B test acceleration (SimGym: traffic-grounded offline testing)
- Adversarial testing (TruthMarketTwin: LLM agents exploit/deceive)
- RL training (Shop-r1: align shopper LLMs via reinforcement learning)
- Privacy-safe synthetic data (BASES: no real user PII needed)

### Critical Gaps

- **Advertiser-side simulation underdeveloped** — most research focuses on user/demand side
- **Calibration**: LLMs converge to "positive average person" (SYN-DIGITS: 50% improvement with correction)
- **Scale**: 1K simulated agents vs billions of real users
- **Validation**: No standard method to prove simulation predicts real A/B outcomes

## LLM Quality for User Behavior Modeling

> **Added:** 2026-06-08 | **Source:** LLMRec (CIKM 2023), LLMRank (ECIR 2024), Argyle (Political Analysis 2023), Homo Silicus (2023), Agent4Rec (SIGIR 2024)

### Verdict: Moderate — Better Than Nothing, Worse Than Specialized Models

LLMs are 5-20% below SASRec/BERT4Rec on sequential prediction accuracy (NDCG/HitRate). They excel in specific niches but have structural limitations.

### Success/Failure Matrix

| Dimension | LLM Quality | Why |
|-----------|:-----------:|-----|
| Cold-start (no history) | **Strong** | World knowledge from pre-training compensates |
| Conversational rec | **Excellent** | Language IS the medium; dominates even without fine-tuning |
| Demographic simulation | **Strong** | "Far beyond surface similarity" (Argyle, 2023) |
| Re-ranking (10-20 items) | **Good** | Comparative judgment via language |
| Sequential prediction | **Moderate** | 5-20% below trained models; can't perceive temporal order |
| Behavioral diversity | **Poor** | "Hyper-accuracy distortion" — converges to average |
| Temporal dynamics | **Poor** | "Struggle to perceive order of interactions" |
| Individual-level prediction | **Poor** | No individual behavioral signal available |
| Impulsive/irrational behavior | **Fails** | RLHF makes models helpful, not realistic |

### The Structural Gap: Declared vs Revealed Preferences

LLMs encode what people *write about* behavior (reviews, forums, guides) but lack what people *actually do* (clicks, dwell times, abandonment, impulse purchases). This declared-vs-revealed preference gap is **structural** — the data that would close it (click logs, session sequences) is proprietary and exactly what makes traditional models work.

### Implications for Simulator Design

1. **Use LLMs for qualitative/demographic simulation** — they capture cultural patterns well
2. **Don't trust LLMs for fine-grained sequential prediction** — use SASRec/BERT4Rec
3. **Calibrate aggressively** — LLMs converge to "positive average person"; SYN-DIGITS shows 50% improvement with correction
4. **Hybrid approach**: LLM for persona/intent modeling + traditional model for sequence/click prediction
5. **Validation is critical**: Always measure Sim2Real gap before trusting simulation results
