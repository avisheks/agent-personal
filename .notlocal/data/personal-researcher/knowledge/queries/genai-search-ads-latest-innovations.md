## Quick Summary by Company

### Google ($403B revenue, 5T+ searches/year)
- **AI Overviews**: 1B+ users; ads integrated; "links get MORE clicks than traditional listings"
- **AI Max**: Keywordless campaigns via Gemini (14% more conversions, 27% for exact match)
- **Direct Offers** (Jan 2026): Native checkout within AI search results
- **Veo/Imagen**: AI video + image generation in Google Ads
- **Smart Bidding Exploration**: RL-based "biggest bidding update in a decade"

### Meta ($201B revenue, 97.8% from ads)
- **Advantage+**: Full AI campaign automation (audience, creative, bid, placement)
- **Gen-AI Creative**: Background gen, image expansion, text variation, full image generation
- **AI Agents for Business**: WhatsApp/Messenger AI for shopping + support (emerging)

### OpenAI ($12B ARR, 20M paid subscribers)
- **ChatGPT Ads** (Mar 2026): $2.5B projected 2026, ambition $100B by 2030
- **Operator** (Jan 2025): Autonomous browser agent completing tasks/purchases
- **Shopping via Stripe** (Sep 2025): In-conversation purchasing (Agentic Commerce Protocol)

### TikTok/ByteDance (~$20B+ ads revenue)
- **Symphony Creative AI** (May 2024): End-to-end AI video (script → avatar → production)
- **Smart+**: Autonomous creative selection, audience, delivery
- **Symphony Dubbing**: AI multilingual localization

### Also Notable
- **Amazon**: AI Image Gen (+40% CTR), Ads Agent (2026), Alexa for Shopping
- **Microsoft**: Brand Agents, Copilot Checkout, "Shortlist Economy" thesis
- **Perplexity**: DISCONTINUED ads (Feb 2026) — trust > ad revenue for answer engines

## Five Structural Shifts

1. **Keywords → Intent**: All platforms moving to objective-based, not keyword-based
2. **Human Creative → AI Production**: Creative direction replaces creative production
3. **Search Ads → Agentic Commerce**: AI agents buy on behalf of users (Operator, Copilot Checkout)
4. **Tracking → AI Contextual**: First-party data + LLM understanding replaces cookies
5. **"Shortlist Economy"**: AI pre-filters 3-5 options; brands not in shortlist are invisible
