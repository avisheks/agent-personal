Based on the provided knowledge pages, the GPT-OSS model family is based on **MoE (Mixture of Experts) architecture**.

The [[Mixture of Experts (MoE)]] page specifically states that "OpenAI's [[GPT-OSS-20B]] and [[GPT-OSS-120B]] models also feature MoE routing mechanisms as part of their architecture, with implementation details available through their technical documentation and model repositories."

Additionally, the same page mentions that "The deployment considerations for MoE models include memory management for multiple experts, efficient routing computation, and load balancing across distributed systems when deploying large-scale MoE architectures like those found in the [[GPT-OSS-120B]] model."

This indicates that both GPT-OSS-20B and GPT-OSS-120B utilize MoE architecture rather than dense architecture, incorporating routing mechanisms and multiple expert networks as part of their design.
