# Return-on-Autonomy (RoA)

**Return-on-Autonomy (RoA)** is a measurement framework that evaluates AI investments based on how they change what an enterprise is capable of, rather than focusing solely on cost savings or traditional return on investment metrics. This approach represents a shift from static financial scorecards to dynamic capability assessment in AI transformation strategies. ^[ai-transformation-predictions-2026.md]

## Overview

RoA differs from conventional AI measurement approaches by focusing on autonomy and capability enhancement rather than purely financial metrics. While traditional AI business cases are often built around cost reduction or efficiency gains, RoA measures the strategic value that AI brings through better decisions, faster insights, new capabilities, and improved customer outcomes. ^[ai-transformation-predictions-2026.md]

## Measurement Philosophy

The RoA framework treats ROI measurement as a learning system rather than a static scorecard designed at the point of initial investment. Organizations implementing this approach continuously refine what they track as they develop a deeper understanding of what AI actually changes in their operations. ^[ai-transformation-predictions-2026.md]

## Implementation Challenges

Many organizations face structural barriers when implementing RoA measurement. CFO and board reporting systems are typically built to receive cost-based business cases, making it difficult to capture strategic value such as enhanced decision-making capabilities, accelerated insight generation, and improved customer outcomes. These benefits require a different measurement architecture than most organizations currently have in place. ^[ai-transformation-predictions-2026.md]

## Organizational Maturity

Research indicates that relatively few organizations have reached the most mature state where AI value reporting actively shapes strategy at the board level. Most organizations remain in earlier stages, measuring value through strategic outcomes, broader business results, or cost reduction alone. Organizations that are pulling ahead in AI transformation are those moving toward RoA-based measurement approaches. ^[ai-transformation-predictions-2026.md]
