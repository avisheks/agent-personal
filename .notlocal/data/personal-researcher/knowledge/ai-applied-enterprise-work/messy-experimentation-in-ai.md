# Messy Experimentation in AI

**Messy Experimentation in AI** refers to an organizational approach that prioritizes broad, exploratory experimentation with artificial intelligence technologies over rigid ROI-focused evaluation in the early stages of AI adoption. This philosophy emphasizes learning and discovery through diverse trials rather than demanding immediate financial justification for AI initiatives.

## Core Philosophy

The concept advocates for treating the current period of AI development as "very early days" where organizations should focus on experimentation rather than return on investment calculations. This approach recognizes that the high rate of innovation in the AI space makes it difficult to predict which experiments will yield transformative results. ^[how-to-maximize-ai-roi-in-2026-ibm.md]

## Key Principles

### Broad Experimentation Over ROI Filtering

The messy experimentation approach encourages organizations to "let a thousand flowers bloom" rather than rigidly filtering ideas based on early ROI metrics. This philosophy suggests that forcing engineers to justify AI work with hard ROI up front is counterproductive, comparing it to asking a child to make a business plan for a hobby. ^[how-to-maximize-ai-roi-in-2026-ibm.md]

### Internal Understanding Development

Rather than relying entirely on third-party service providers, organizations are encouraged to develop their own understanding of AI infrastructure. This hands-on approach allows companies to truly grasp what works and what doesn't in their specific context. ^[how-to-maximize-ai-roi-in-2026-ibm.md]

### Long-term Perspective

The approach requires leaders to obtain widespread buy-in across the organization with an understanding that ROI might be years away. This long-term view acknowledges that trying to force control through short-term ROI evaluation risks missing transformative opportunities. ^[how-to-maximize-ai-roi-in-2026-ibm.md]

## Implementation Challenges

### Stakeholder Buy-in

One of the primary challenges of messy experimentation is convincing financial stakeholders to support initiatives without immediate ROI justification. Organizations must balance the need for fiscal responsibility with the potential for breakthrough innovations that may not be immediately quantifiable. ^[how-to-maximize-ai-roi-in-2026-ibm.md]

### Resource Allocation

The approach requires careful consideration of how to allocate resources across multiple experimental initiatives while maintaining operational efficiency and meeting existing business objectives.

## Industry Perspective

The messy experimentation philosophy has been advocated by prominent industry leaders, particularly in the context of the rapidly evolving AI landscape. The approach reflects the understanding that the transformative potential of AI technologies may not be fully realized through traditional business evaluation methods in the early stages of adoption. ^[how-to-maximize-ai-roi-in-2026-ibm.md]
