# Knowledge Base Index

## General
- [Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)](direct-preference-optimization-dpo.md) — A training method that directly optimizes language models to prefer better responses over worse ones using pairwise preference data without requiring a separate reward model.
- [Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)](group-relative-policy-optimization-grpo.md) — A policy optimization technique that improves model performance by comparing and optimizing relative to groups of sampled outputs rather than individual responses.
- [Hugging Face TRL Library](hugging-face-trl-library.md) — A comprehensive toolkit that provides trainer abstractions and implementations for SFT, DPO, PPO, and GRPO training methods in a unified ecosystem.
- [InstructGPT Training Pipeline](instructgpt-training-pipeline.md) — The canonical three-stage training approach consisting of supervised fine-tuning, reward model training, and reinforcement learning from human feedback.
- [Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)](reinforcement-learning-from-human-feedback-rlhf.md) — A training paradigm that uses human preference data to train a reward model, which then guides policy optimization through reinforcement learning methods like PPO.
- [Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)](supervised-fine-tuning-sft.md) — A training method that teaches language models to imitate high-quality demonstrations through maximum likelihood estimation on input-output pairs.
