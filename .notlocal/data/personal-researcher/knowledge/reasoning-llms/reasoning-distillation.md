# Reasoning Distillation

**Reasoning distillation** is a machine learning technique for training smaller, more efficient models to replicate the reasoning capabilities of larger, more powerful language models. This approach involves using reasoning traces generated by frontier models to teach smaller models how to perform step-by-step logical reasoning without requiring the computational resources of the original large model.

## Overview

Reasoning distillation represents a key stage in the modern pipeline for developing reasoning-capable AI systems. Rather than training reasoning models from scratch, most successful implementations follow a multi-stage approach where distillation serves as a critical intermediate step between initial model training and deployment. ^[chatgpt-reasoning-llms.md]

The technique addresses the practical challenge that training reasoning models "from scratch" is usually the wrong framing for most applications. Instead, the industry trend follows a structured pipeline: pretrain base model, apply [[supervised-fine-tuning-sft]] on reasoning traces, perform RL reasoning optimization, distill into smaller models, and finally integrate with agent/tool systems. ^[chatgpt-reasoning-llms.md]

## Training Process

### Source Model Selection

Reasoning distillation typically uses traces from frontier reasoning models such as:
- [[qwen3-language-model]] family models
- [[deepseek-r1-reasoning-model]]
- GPT-OSS models

These larger models serve as "teachers" that generate high-quality reasoning traces for the distillation process. ^[chatgpt-reasoning-llms.md]

### Distillation Methodology

The distillation process involves training smaller models on reasoning traces generated by the larger source models. This approach allows the smaller models to learn structured reasoning patterns without requiring the same computational overhead as the original models. The technique focuses on teaching the smaller model to replicate the step-by-step logical reasoning demonstrated in the traces. ^[chatgpt-reasoning-llms.md]

## Advantages and Applications

### Efficiency Benefits

Reasoning distillation offers significant practical advantages over training large reasoning models directly. The resulting smaller models can perform reasoning tasks with substantially reduced computational requirements while maintaining much of the reasoning capability of their larger counterparts. ^[chatgpt-reasoning-llms.md]

### Enterprise Applications

For enterprise systems, reasoning distillation represents the highest ROI phase in developing reasoning capabilities. This is particularly relevant for domain-specific applications such as advertising systems, commerce platforms, and other enterprise AI implementations where computational efficiency is crucial. ^[chatgpt-reasoning-llms.md]

## Relationship to Other Techniques

Reasoning distillation typically follows [[supervised-fine-tuning-sft]] on reasoning traces and precedes more advanced techniques like [[chain-of-thought-reasoning]] optimization through reinforcement learning methods. The distilled models often serve as the foundation for agentic reasoning systems that incorporate tool use, retrieval, and planning capabilities. ^[chatgpt-reasoning-llms.md]

Most successful reasoning systems are described as "distilled, reinforced, verifier-trained, and heavily scaffolded" rather than trained from zero, highlighting the central role of distillation in the modern reasoning model development pipeline. ^[chatgpt-reasoning-llms.md]

## Implementation Pipeline

The standard implementation follows a four-stage process:

1. **Base Model Pretraining** - Initial language model development
2. **[[supervised-fine-tuning-sft]]** - Training on reasoning traces from larger models
3. **RL Reasoning Optimization** - Further refinement using reinforcement learning techniques
4. **Agent Integration** - Incorporation of tool use, retrieval, and planning systems

This structured approach allows teams to build reasoning capabilities incrementally rather than attempting to develop them from scratch. ^[chatgpt-reasoning-llms.md]

## Recent Developments

Recent work has focused on learning to reason with traces from models like GPT-OSS and [[deepseek-r1-reasoning-model]], demonstrating the practical viability of reasoning distillation for creating efficient reasoning systems. The technique has become particularly important as organizations seek to deploy reasoning capabilities at scale without the computational overhead of frontier models. ^[chatgpt-reasoning-llms.md]
