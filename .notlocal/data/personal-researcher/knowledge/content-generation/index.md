# Knowledge Base Index

## General
- [Adoption Ceiling Problem](adoption-ceiling-problem.md) — The phenomenon where advertisers only adopt the top 30-50 of 200+ AI-generated suggestions due to cognitive overload and trust constraints, making adoption the primary bottleneck rather than generation quality.
- [Constitutional AI for Ads](constitutional-ai-for-ads.md) — A policy alignment approach that uses AI systems to enforce advertising content policies and brand safety requirements without extensive human labeling.
- [Critic-Reranker Pipeline](critic-reranker-pipeline.md) — A multi-stage architecture where generated content passes through a critic model for quality assessment followed by a performance-based reranker before presentation to users.
- [Double-Randomized Experimentation](double-randomized-experimentation.md) — An experimental design that randomizes both at the advertiser level and marketplace level to isolate causal effects from marketplace spillover in advertising platforms.
- [Factual Grounding Rate](factual-grounding-rate.md) — A quality metric measuring the percentage of generated content claims that can be traced back to verified product attributes in the catalog data.
- [Intent-Level Abstraction](intent-level-abstraction.md) — A design pattern that groups individual keywords into semantic intent clusters (e.g., 'Performance Running', 'Comfort/Lifestyle') to reduce cognitive load and increase advertiser adoption rates.
- [LLM-as-Judge Quality Scoring](llm-as-judge-quality-scoring.md) — Using a second LLM to evaluate generated content for quality, relevance, brand alignment, and policy compliance as part of a multi-tier validation pipeline.
- [Zero-Shot Locale Expansion](zero-shot-locale-expansion.md) — A strategy for expanding content generation to new locales using multilingual LLMs with locale-specific instructions rather than translation, capturing local search behavior patterns.
