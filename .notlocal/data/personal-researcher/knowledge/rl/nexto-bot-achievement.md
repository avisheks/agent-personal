# Nexto Bot Achievement

**Nexto Bot** is a reinforcement learning-powered artificial intelligence bot that achieved Grand Champion 1 rank in Rocket League, representing one of the highest-performing AI agents in competitive gaming. Developed as the second iteration in the Necto bot series, Nexto demonstrated exceptional performance across multiple game modes in 2023. ^[rlgym-rocket-league-rl-environment.md]

## Performance Rankings

Nexto achieved Grand Champion 1 status across all major Rocket League competitive modes, placing it among the top percentiles of all players:

- **1v1 (Duel)**: Top 0.12% of players
- **2v2 (Doubles)**: Top 0.95% of players  
- **3v3 (Standard)**: Top 0.46% of players

These rankings represent elite-level performance, with Grand Champion 1 being one of the highest competitive tiers in Rocket League's ranking system. ^[rlgym-rocket-league-rl-environment.md]

## Technical Development

Nexto was developed using the [[RLGym — Rocket League Reinforcement Learning Environment]], which provides a modular, OpenAI Gym-inspired interface for training reinforcement learning agents in Rocket League. The bot utilized a combination of [[Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)]] techniques and behavioral cloning from human gameplay replays. ^[rlgym-rocket-league-rl-environment.md]

The training approach combined imitation learning with reinforcement learning, drawing inspiration from OpenAI's Video Pre-Training (VPT) methodology. This hybrid approach allowed Nexto to learn both from human demonstrations and through direct interaction with the game environment. ^[rlgym-rocket-league-rl-environment.md]

## Bot Series Evolution

Nexto represents the second generation in a series of increasingly sophisticated Rocket League AI bots:

- **Necto (V1, 2022)**: Achieved Diamond rank and won the 2022 RLBot Championship
- **Nexto (V2, 2023)**: Reached Grand Champion 1 across all game modes
- **Tecko (V3)**: Development cancelled due to "lack of improvement" over Nexto

This progression demonstrates the challenges of continued performance gains in competitive gaming AI, where Nexto appears to have reached a performance plateau that subsequent iterations could not surpass. ^[rlgym-rocket-league-rl-environment.md]

## Competitive Context

Nexto's achievements occurred within the broader [[RLGym — Rocket League Reinforcement Learning Environment]] ecosystem, which includes the RLBot community framework for custom bots and organized tournaments such as the RLBot Championship. The bot's performance represents a significant milestone in applying [[reinforcement-learning-from-human-feedback-rlhf]] techniques to competitive gaming scenarios. ^[rlgym-rocket-league-rl-environment.md]

The development team's decision to discontinue the Tecko (V3) project after Nexto suggests that the Grand Champion 1 level may represent a practical ceiling for current RL techniques in this domain, highlighting the challenges of achieving superhuman performance in complex, real-time competitive environments. ^[rlgym-rocket-league-rl-environment.md]
