# OpenAI Five Dota 2 Champion

**OpenAI Five** was a multi-agent reinforcement learning system developed by OpenAI that achieved professional-level performance in the complex multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game Dota 2. The system represents one of the most significant achievements in multi-agent AI, culminating in victories against world champion teams in 2019. ^[multi-agent-rl-ecosystems-and-advertising-applications.md]

## Overview

OpenAI Five was designed to play Dota 2, a real-time strategy game where two teams of five players compete to destroy the opposing team's base structure called the "Ancient." The game involves complex strategic decision-making, long-term planning, team coordination, and real-time tactical execution across a large action space. ^[multi-agent-rl-ecosystems-and-advertising-applications.md]

## Technical Architecture

The system utilized [[multi-agent-orchestration-architecture]] with five separate but coordinated AI agents, each controlling one hero character on the team. The training process employed [[reinforcement-learning-from-human-feedback-rlhf]] techniques combined with self-play mechanisms to develop sophisticated strategies and coordination patterns. ^[multi-agent-rl-ecosystems-and-advertising-applications.md]

## Training Infrastructure

OpenAI Five required massive computational resources for training, utilizing 128,000 CPUs and 256 GPUs simultaneously. The system accumulated the equivalent of 180 years of gameplay experience per day during peak training periods, demonstrating the scale necessary for achieving superhuman performance in complex multi-agent environments. ^[multi-agent-rl-ecosystems-and-advertising-applications.md]

## Competitive Achievements

In 2019, OpenAI Five achieved its most notable success by defeating the professional Dota 2 team OG, who were the reigning world champions at The International tournament. This victory marked a significant milestone in AI gaming, as it demonstrated that artificial intelligence could master a game requiring complex team coordination, long-term strategic planning, and real-time decision-making against the world's best human players. ^[multi-agent-rl-ecosystems-and-advertising-applications.md]

## Significance in Multi-Agent AI

OpenAI Five stands alongside other landmark achievements in multi-agent reinforcement learning, including [[deepmind-alphastar]] in StarCraft II and various [[hide-and-seek]] experiments. The system demonstrated that [[multi-agent-rl]] techniques could scale to complex, real-world-like scenarios with multiple interacting agents, emergent strategies, and sophisticated coordination requirements. ^[multi-agent-rl-ecosystems-and-advertising-applications.md]

## Legacy and Impact

The success of OpenAI Five has influenced subsequent research in multi-agent systems and demonstrated the potential for AI to master complex collaborative tasks. The project contributed to the broader understanding of how [[reinforcement-learning-for-reasoning]] and multi-agent coordination can be applied to challenging real-world problems beyond gaming, including applications in [[gen-ai-applied-to-search-and-advertising]] and other domains requiring sophisticated multi-agent coordination. ^[multi-agent-rl-ecosystems-and-advertising-applications.md]
