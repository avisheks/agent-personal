# Project Glasswing

**Project Glasswing** is Anthropic's restricted access program for their most advanced AI model, Claude Mythos 5, launched on June 9, 2026. The project provides invitation-only access to approximately 150 organizations across 15+ countries for autonomous cybersecurity vulnerability discovery and biology research applications. ^[anthropic-model-family-comparison.md]

## Overview

Project Glasswing represents Anthropic's approach to controlled deployment of frontier AI capabilities. The program grants access to [[Claude Mythos 5]], which demonstrates unprecedented performance in autonomous cybersecurity research, achieving 93.9% on [[SWE-bench Verified]] and discovering over 10,000 high and critical vulnerabilities, including a 27-year-old OpenBSD bug. ^[anthropic-model-family-comparison.md]

## Access and Restrictions

Access to Project Glasswing is strictly controlled through an invitation-only system. The program currently includes approximately 150 organizations distributed across more than 15 countries, focusing on entities with legitimate cybersecurity and biological research needs. ^[anthropic-model-family-comparison.md]

The restricted access model contrasts with Anthropic's general availability approach for other models in their family, such as [[Claude Fable 5]], which uses the same base model as Mythos but includes safety routing that redirects cybersecurity and biology queries to the less capable [[Claude Opus 4.8]]. ^[anthropic-model-family-comparison.md]

## Technical Capabilities

Project Glasswing provides access to Claude Mythos 5's full autonomous capabilities, including:

- **Autonomous vulnerability discovery**: The model can independently identify security flaws in major operating systems and browsers without human guidance
- **Advanced reasoning**: 94.6% performance on GPQA Diamond and 83.1% on CyberGym benchmarks
- **Extended context processing**: 1M token input capacity with 128K token output
- **Multi-domain expertise**: Specialized performance in both cybersecurity and biological research domains ^[anthropic-model-family-comparison.md]

## Pricing Structure

Project Glasswing operates on a premium pricing model at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens during general availability, with higher rates of $25/$125 during preview and research phases. This represents the highest pricing tier in Anthropic's model family hierarchy. ^[anthropic-model-family-comparison.md]

## Safety Considerations

The restricted nature of Project Glasswing reflects Anthropic's approach to managing potentially sensitive AI capabilities. Unlike the publicly available [[Claude Fable 5]], which implements safety routing to redirect high-risk queries, Project Glasswing participants receive direct access to the model's full autonomous capabilities for legitimate research purposes. ^[anthropic-model-family-comparison.md]

## Related Models

Project Glasswing sits at the top of Anthropic's model hierarchy: Mythos > [[Claude Fable 5]] > [[Claude Opus 4.8]] > [[Claude Sonnet 4.6]] > [[Claude Haiku 4.5]]. The program demonstrates Anthropic's strategy of developing advanced capabilities while maintaining controlled access through invitation-only programs before broader deployment. ^[anthropic-model-family-comparison.md]
