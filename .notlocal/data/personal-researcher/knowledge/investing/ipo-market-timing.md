# IPO Market Timing and Performance

Markets are typically **strong** before major IPOs. The claim that "markets tank before big IPOs" is contradicted by historical data. Companies and underwriters deliberately time IPOs for favorable market conditions — the "IPO window." ^[ipo-market-performance-relationship.md]

## Pre-IPO Market Performance

Average S&P 500 returns before the 6 largest U.S. IPOs (2012-2023):
- 30 days before: **+0.5%** (flat)
- 60 days before: **+2.0%** (positive)
- 90 days before: **+4.6%** (solidly positive)

Every mega-IPO in the sample occurred during a bull market or at/near all-time highs. ^[ipo-market-performance-relationship.md]

## IPO Volume and Market Conditions

IPO activity surges in strong markets and collapses in weak ones:
- Bear market (2008): 62 IPOs
- Bull market peak (2021): 1,035 IPOs (all-time record)
- Bear market (2022): 181 IPOs (83% decline from 2021)

Academic research (Loughran & Ritter): positive correlation between IPO volume and market level in 14 of 15 countries studied. ^[ipo-market-performance-relationship.md]

## Liquidity Drain Effect

Mega-IPOs ($10-25B) can cause sector-level rotation but rarely drive broad market declines:
- Total U.S. equity market cap: $40-50T
- Largest IPO absorption: $25.6B (Saudi Aramco) = 0.05% of market
- Sector effects documented: Zynga -13.4% after Facebook IPO; Amazon -2.1% after Alibaba

JPMorgan estimates a theoretical $2T SpaceX IPO could force ~$950B in passive fund rebalancing — but this would be unprecedented in scale. ^[ipo-market-performance-relationship.md]
