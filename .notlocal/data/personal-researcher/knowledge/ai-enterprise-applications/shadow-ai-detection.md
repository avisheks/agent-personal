# Shadow AI Detection

**Shadow AI Detection** refers to the identification and monitoring of unauthorized or unmanaged artificial intelligence systems, models, and applications that operate within an organization without proper governance, security oversight, or compliance controls. This practice has become increasingly critical as enterprises adopt AI technologies at scale while maintaining regulatory compliance and security standards.

## Overview

Shadow AI represents AI systems that exist outside of an organization's formal AI governance framework. These systems may include unauthorized model deployments, unmanaged AI applications, or AI tools used by employees without proper security controls. The detection of such systems is essential for maintaining comprehensive AI governance and ensuring that all AI operations adhere to relevant laws and standards.

Good governance drives adoption, as people want to use AI they trust. Proactive risk detection and mitigation, enhanced accuracy, responsible model management and compliance with regulations are all improved through automated governance and security measures. This approach ensures that risks are identified and addressed before they escalate, models are governed effectively and all operations adhere to relevant laws and standards. ^[building-robust-framework-data-ai-governance-security-ibm.md]

## Integration with AI Governance

Shadow AI detection systems integrate seamlessly with broader [[AI Governance]] frameworks to provide comprehensive visibility across an organization's AI landscape. When shadow AI is detected, it can be automatically aligned with appropriate use cases and subjected to the relevant risk and compliance controls within the governance system.

For example, when detection systems identify shadow AI, it appears in governance platforms aligned with the appropriate use case, and the appropriate risk and compliance controls are applied. This enables governance and security teams to view the same AI inventory and AI risk for trustworthy AI. ^[building-robust-framework-data-ai-governance-security-ibm.md]

## Cross-Platform Governance

Modern shadow AI detection supports cross-platform governance, risk management and compliance for generative AI models across multiple cloud providers and open source communities. This approach provides centralized visibility and transparency in one solution, enabling organizations to maintain oversight regardless of where AI systems are deployed. ^[building-robust-framework-data-ai-governance-security-ibm.md]

## Security Integration

Shadow AI detection systems integrate with security platforms to provide a unified risk and governance solution. This integration allows disparate teams to view a single set of metrics for both business and security risks, linking security incidents to business risk and enabling responsible AI scaling while balancing productivity with accountability and trust. ^[building-robust-framework-data-ai-governance-security-ibm.md]

## Enterprise Benefits

Organizations implementing shadow AI detection can:

- Safeguard [[Agentic AI]] at scale
- Navigate complex regulations with confidence
- Achieve measurable success through strong governance and security
- Build trust through comprehensive AI asset management
- Detect vulnerabilities and customize security policies
- Ensure regulatory compliance across all AI operations

The future of AI depends on trust, which can be built only on a foundation of responsible governance and robust security. By adopting unified shadow AI detection strategies, enterprises can scale AI confidently with the knowledge that their systems are secure, compliant and aligned with ethical standards. ^[building-robust-framework-data-ai-governance-security-ibm.md]
