# AI Overviews in Search

AI Overviews is Google's generative AI-powered search feature that provides synthesized answers at the top of search results pages. Originally launched as Search Generative Experience (SGE), the feature was rebranded to AI Overviews in May 2024 and has become a central component of Google's AI-driven search strategy. ^[gen-ai-search-ads-2024-2026.md]

## Overview

AI Overviews uses Google's [[Gemini]] language models to generate comprehensive responses to search queries by synthesizing information from multiple web sources. The feature appears at the top of search results and provides users with direct answers while still including links to source websites. By the end of 2024, AI Overviews reached over 1 billion users globally following its initial US rollout in May 2024. ^[gen-ai-search-ads-2024-2026.md]

## Evolution and Timeline

The feature began as Search Generative Experience (SGE) before being rebranded to AI Overviews in May 2024. The rollout followed a phased approach, starting with US users and expanding globally throughout 2024. Google reported that "links in AI Overviews get more clicks than traditional web listings," suggesting the feature enhances rather than cannibalizes traditional search traffic. ^[gen-ai-search-ads-2024-2026.md]

## Integration with Advertising

### Initial Ad Integration (2025)

In 2025, Google began integrating advertisements directly into AI Overviews, marking a significant shift in how search advertising operates. This integration allows advertisers to reach users within the AI-generated content itself rather than only in traditional search result listings. ^[gen-ai-search-ads-2024-2026.md]

### Direct Offers Pilot (2026)

In January 2026, Google launched a "Direct Offers" pilot program that enables promotion bundling and native checkout functionality within AI Overviews. This feature allows users to complete purchases directly from the AI Overview interface without navigating to external websites. ^[gen-ai-search-ads-2024-2026.md]

### New Ad Formats for AI Mode

By mid-2026, Google introduced four new Gemini-powered ad formats specifically designed for [[AI Mode in Search]]:
- Conversational Discovery Ads
- Highlighted Answers
- AI Shopping Ads  
- Business Agent for Leads

These formats are optimized for the conversational, multi-turn research experience that AI Mode provides. ^[gen-ai-search-ads-2024-2026.md]

## Relationship to AI Mode

AI Overviews works in conjunction with Google's [[AI Mode in Search]], which was announced in May 2025. While AI Overviews provides synthesized answers for individual queries, AI Mode enables conversational, multi-turn research sessions. Both features leverage Google's Gemini models and represent Google's broader strategy to transform search from a keyword-based system to an AI-powered conversational interface. ^[gen-ai-search-ads-2024-2026.md]

## Impact on Search Behavior

Google reports that 75% of users make faster and more confident decisions when using AI-powered search features like AI Overviews and AI Mode. This suggests that the synthesized, comprehensive answers provided by AI Overviews help users process information more efficiently than traditional search result lists. ^[gen-ai-search-ads-2024-2026.md]

## Technical Implementation

AI Overviews is powered by Google's Gemini language models, which analyze search queries and synthesize responses from multiple web sources. The system is designed to maintain source attribution while providing coherent, comprehensive answers that address user intent beyond simple keyword matching. ^[gen-ai-search-ads-2024-2026.md]

## Industry Context

AI Overviews represents part of a broader industry trend toward AI-powered search experiences. Similar approaches include [[ChatGPT Search]] from OpenAI and various answer engines like Perplexity AI. However, Google's integration of advertising into AI Overviews distinguishes it from competitors who have struggled to monetize AI-powered search effectively. ^[gen-ai-search-ads-2024-2026.md]
