## Five Structural Shifts

1. **Keywords → Intent**: Google's "keywordless technology" (AI Max) learns from landing pages and creative, not keyword lists. All platforms moving to objective-based optimization.

2. **Human Creative → AI Production**: Amazon 40% CTR lift from AI images. Google Veo/Imagen. Meta full image gen. TikTok Symphony script-to-video. Human role shifts to creative direction, not production.

3. **Search Ads → Agentic Commerce**: AI agents (Operator, Copilot Checkout, Direct Offers) complete purchases without traditional search. "Shortlist economy" means AI pre-filters options before human sees them.

4. **Third-Party Tracking → AI Contextual**: Cookie deprecation + privacy laws drive first-party data + LLM-based contextual understanding. Performance Max/Advantage+ rely on conversion signals, not user tracking.

5. **Ad-Supported Answers Under Pressure**: Perplexity DISCONTINUED ads (Feb 2026) to preserve trust. Google maintains ads work in AI Overviews. OpenAI projecting $100B by 2030. Model viability varies by product type.

## Cautionary Signal: Perplexity's Advertising Reversal

Perplexity AI tested CPM-based ads (Jul 2024 - Feb 2026), then **discontinued advertising entirely** in favor of subscription-first ($200M ARR). Reason: "preserving user trust in the answer engine." This suggests ad monetization can undermine the core value proposition of unbiased AI synthesis — not every AI product can be ad-supported.
