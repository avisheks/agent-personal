# Gen-AI in Search and Ads: Key Innovations (2024-2026)

## Google

### AI Overviews (formerly SGE)
- May 2024: US rollout; end 2024: 1B+ users globally
- 2025: Ads integrated into AI Overviews
- Jan 2026: "Direct Offers" pilot (promotion bundling, native checkout)
- Mid-2026: Four new Gemini-powered ad formats for AI Mode
- Impact: "links in AI Overviews get more clicks than traditional web listings"

### AI Mode in Search
- Announced May 2025 (Google Marketing Live)
- Conversational multi-turn research with Google Search
- 75% report faster, more confident decisions
- New ad formats: Conversational Discovery Ads, Highlighted Answers, AI Shopping Ads, Business Agent for Leads

### AI Max for Search Campaigns
- Global beta May 2025
- Gemini-based "keywordless technology" — learns from keywords, creative, landing pages
- 14% more conversions at similar CPA; L'Oreal: 2X conversion at 31% lower cost
- 27% uplift for exact/phrase match campaigns

### Performance Max + Gen-AI Creative
- Imagen 3 (image), Veo (video), Gemini (text) integrated into Google Ads
- India "Generated for you" feature in Product Studio (2025)
- Channel performance reporting added (2026)

### Smart Bidding Exploration
- "Biggest bidding update in over a decade" (May 2025)
- RL-based bid optimization for less obvious, high-performing searches

### Revenue: Alphabet $350B (2024), $403B (2025); 5T+ searches/year; ~86% from ads

## Meta

### Advantage+ Suite
- AI-automated campaigns: audience, bidding, placement, creative
- Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns, Creative, Audience
- Deep learning + RL for optimization

### Gen-AI Creative Tools (GA 2023-2025)
- Background Generation (diffusion models)
- Image Expansion (aspect ratio fitting)
- Text Variation (LLM-generated copy alternatives)
- Full Image Generation (text-to-image, expanded 2024)

### Revenue: $164.5B (2024), $201B (2025), 22% YoY growth; 97.8% from advertising

## OpenAI

### ChatGPT Search
- Oct-Dec 2024: Full deployment
- Real-time web search + synthesis in conversations
- Available to all users (free + paid)

### Ads in ChatGPT
- Jan 17, 2026: Testing announced (free tier, US adults)
- Mar 2026: Ads visible to users
- Projected: $2.5B ad revenue 2026; $100B by 2030

### Shopping (Stripe partnership, Sep 2025)
- In-conversation commerce via Agentic Commerce Protocol
- Initially Etsy purchases for US users
- Commission-based revenue

### Operator (Jan 23, 2025)
- Autonomous browser-based task completion
- Computer-Using Agent (CUA) in controlled VM
- Pro subscribers US initially

### Revenue: $3.7B ARR (2024) → $12B (Jul 2025); 20M paid subscribers

## ByteDance / TikTok

### Symphony Creative AI (May 2024)
- End-to-end AI video creation
- AI scripting assistant, dubbing, digital avatars
- LLMs + video synthesis + RL-based creative optimization

### Smart+ Performance Automation
- Advertisers input assets/budget/objectives
- TikTok autonomously selects creative, audience, delivery

### TikTok One
- Unified creative hub: 2M creators + agency partners + AI tools

### Revenue: $14.15B projected 2023; estimated $20B+ for 2024-2025

- Rufus → Alexa for Shopping (rebranded May 2026): conversational shopping assistant

### Microsoft
- Performance Max launched (2024-2025)
- Copilot in Advertising (productivity tool)
- 2026 Strategy: Brand Agents, Copilot Checkout, Unified Commerce Protocols
- "Shortlist Economy" thesis: AI pre-filters 3-5 options; brands not in shortlist never reach humans
- Warning: "60% of ad spend targets on-site search — vulnerable if discovery migrates to AI"

## Cross-Cutting Themes

1. Keywords → Intent: Google AI Max "keywordless"; all platforms moving to objective-based
2. Creative automation: Amazon 40% CTR lift; Google Veo/Imagen; Meta full image gen; TikTok Symphony
3. Agentic commerce: OpenAI Operator, ChatGPT+Stripe, Microsoft Copilot Checkout, Google Direct Offers
4. Privacy → AI contextual: First-party data + AI modeling replaces third-party tracking
5. Threat to search ads: Zero-click AI answers + agent disintermediation
6. Perplexity reversal: Ads can undermine trust in answer engines
7. "Shortlist economy": AI agents pre-filter; brands must optimize for algorithm recommendation, not human persuasion
