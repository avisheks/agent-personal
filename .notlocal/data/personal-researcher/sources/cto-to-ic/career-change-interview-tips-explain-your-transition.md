## Real Success Story: Josh's Military-to-Tech Transition

Josh's career pivot was one of the most challenging: transitioning from military service to tech. He had years of leadership experience, complex problem-solving skills, and team management under pressure. But he faced a critical challenge: **translation**.

How do you explain "coordinated multi-unit operations" to a startup founder?

**Josh's approach:** Deliberate practice. He practiced reframing his military accomplishments 15+ times:

❌ **Before:** "Managed logistics for multi-unit operations ensuring mission-critical equipment deployment"

✅ **After:** "Coordinated cross-functional teams across locations to deliver critical resources on tight deadlines with changing requirements"

After dozens of practice sessions, the translation became automatic. The framing got sharper. The confidence grew.

**The result?** Josh landed his tech role.
