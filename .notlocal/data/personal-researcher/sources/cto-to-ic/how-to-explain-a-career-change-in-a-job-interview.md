Explaining a career change during a job interview requires honesty, clarity, and emphasis on transferrable skills and motivations. Start by highlighting the reasons behind the career shift, focusing on positive aspects like personal growth, a passion for learning, or a desire for new challenges. Emphasize how your previous experiences equipped you with skills relevant to the new role, showcasing transferable skills such as adaptability, problem-solving, leadership, or communication skills. Connect the dots between your past roles and the desired position, demonstrating how your diverse background adds unique value to the prospective employer.
