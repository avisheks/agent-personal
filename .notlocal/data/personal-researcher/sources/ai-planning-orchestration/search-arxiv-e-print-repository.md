As evidence, we introduce CARMA, a Layer 2 prototype for autonomous ETL agents in which one specification drives enforcement, evaluation, and escalation, with every decision traceable to a versioned specification, naming what AI oversight is missing and giving independent teams principles to build the missing pieces so they compose. △ Less
